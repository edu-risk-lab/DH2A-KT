A credit protocol validates auxiliary knowledge in knowledge tracing.
Each message gets one of three outcomes against a designated control.
Primary rule: one-sided paired t on held-out test AUC at +0.002, Holm/BH.
XES3G5M LSTM: time package credited on three folds; aligned gap is not.
No confirmatory ASSIST2012 or attention-encoder contrast is credited.
Credit labels can differ by encoder, recipe, and timestamp meaning.
