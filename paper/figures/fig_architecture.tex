\caption{DH$^2$A-KT is an attribution pipeline, not a single predictor.
Train-only knowledge is constructed and audited, then compared in a
laddered ablation with capacity-matched, architecture-matched, and
package-level controls (Table~\ref{tab:evidence-types}).
The Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ \emph{package} meets the five-seed gate
on XES3G5M as a package-level ablation (not capacity-matched);
architecture-matched aligned-gap controls stay positive
($+0.00183$ vs.\ T-zero; $+0.00144$ vs.\ T-misaligned) but below the
$+0.002$ rule and are not separately credited.
The question--KC incidence message does not meet the capacity-matched
gate.
Teacher grouping and per-concept exponential forgetting are $0/5$.
Remaining HypergraphConv/metadata forms in the dashed box are
single-seed diagnostics. QKC-T is the observed-incidence evaluation arm;
Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal positive control.
The dashed graph-only encoder and the exploratory Critic survey are not
part of this credited map (\ref{app:graph-only}; Supplementary Material).}
