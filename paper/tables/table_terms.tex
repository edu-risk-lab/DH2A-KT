Term & Meaning \\
\midrule
Message & One auxiliary input placed under test. A component is the same object \\
On arm & The arm that contains the message \\
Control & The comparison arm. It differs from the on arm only as the rung specifies \\
Rung & Capacity-matched message, architecture-matched input, or package \\
Package & A component added together with the maps that read it \\
Aligned gap input & The observed $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$, with its map held fixed \\
T-zero & That map with every gap set to $0$ \\
T-misaligned & That map with gaps permuted inside each learner \\
T-shuffled & That map with gaps permuted across learners \\
T-boundary & That map fed only $1[\Delta t{=}0]$ \\
Reference recipe & The training recipe of the XES3G5M ladder \\
Corpus configuration & The recipe stored with that corpus \\
Seed rule & Every paired seed difference is at least $+2.0\times 10^{-3}$ \\
Interval rule & The lower 95\% bound of the mean paired difference is at least $+2.0\times 10^{-3}$ \\
