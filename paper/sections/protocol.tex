\section{Credit protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

The protocol decides whether a knowledge message may be credited for a
change in next-step AUC on a given corpus and backbone. It does not
define a predictor. The tracer of Section~\ref{sec:encoding} is the
backbone on which it is run here, and Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture}
summarises the pipeline. Table~\ref{tab:terms} fixes the terms used below.

\subsection{Three rungs}
\label{sec:protocol-rungs}
Each contrast is placed on one rung before it is run
(Table~\ref{tab:evidence-types}).

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{(i) Capacity-matched message.} The on and off arms share
  every map; only the message content changes. The question--KC
  incidence control, which replaces the train-only incidence matrix by
  zeros, is this rung.
  \item \textbf{(ii) Architecture-matched input.} The maps stay and the
  input is replaced. For the gap this means T-zero (every gap set to
  $0$) and T-misaligned (gaps permuted within each learner), with
  T-shuffled (gaps permuted across learners) and T-boundary (only
  $1[\Delta t{=}0]$) as diagnostic variants. This is the
  strongest available control for an input, because the control arm
  keeps a map that receives gradient.
  \item \textbf{(iii) Package.} The component is added together with
  its maps, and the number of added parameters is recorded. A pass here
  credits the package, not its input. The linear
  $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch is this rung: one
  $\mathrm{Linear}(1,128)$ with bias (256 parameters) absent from the
  no-time arm.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Families, metric, and the primary rule}
\label{sec:protocol-threshold}
Contrasts are assigned to one of three analysis families recorded in a
dated repository note
(Data Availability Statement). Family membership and the decision rule
were fixed for a retrospective reanalysis of the collected ten-seed
runs after the earlier interval/seed map was already known; they are
not an outcome-blind preregistration before data collection, and the
family~C map is not a confirmatory test on unused data. A later dated
note (5~November~2026) locks follow-up runs that were not used to
choose $\tau$ or the rule.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Family C (primary analysis family).} The primary claims
  of this paper under attempt-onset inputs: zero-incidence package
  versus no time; aligned gap versus T-zero; and capacity-matched
  question--KC incidence, each on the LSTM reference recipe across
  corpora and folds where run, plus the same three time and incidence
  contrasts under the attention encoder on XES3G5M (second encoder, same
  corpus). Size: $29$ contrasts after Junyi attempt-onset inputs that
  fail the availability audit were moved to family~D
  (Section~\ref{sec:junyi-onset-audit}).
  \item \textbf{Family E (exploratory).} Secondary or sensitivity
  contrasts: history-only Junyi gaps on the $50{,}000$-learner subsample
  and on all-learner folds~0--2 (LSTM and attention encoder);
  attention-encoder time contrasts on ASSISTments (second encoder on a
  second corpus under the untuned LSTM recipe); the Junyi seconds
  unit check (Section~8.1); observed-incidence
  packages; the ASSISTments $1{,}000$\,s export; and other
  architecture-matched gap variants beyond T-zero.
  \item \textbf{Family D (diagnostic, no credit).} Contrasts that use an
  input unavailable at prediction time: Junyi completion-time query
  gaps (microseconds and seconds), the synthetic leakage simulation, and the blocked Junyi onset
  rebuild (Section~\ref{sec:junyi-onset-audit}).
\end{itemize}

The primary metric is \emph{pooled held-out evaluation AUC}: AUC on the
union of the fold's original validation and original test learners
(field \texttt{dh2\_kt\_auc} in the run CSVs), at the checkpoint chosen
by early stopping on an internal selection split carved from training
learners only ($\mathrm{val\_frac}{=}0.1$). That pooled evaluation set
is never used for checkpoint selection. Where a table reports a true
original-test-only rescore (different $n$ of scored positions), the
caption says so explicitly. For a fixed set $S$ of paired training seeds
($|S|{=}10$ in this paper), let $\Delta_s$ be the pooled evaluation AUC
of the on arm minus that of the designated control under seed $s$, with
sample mean $\bar\Delta$ and sample standard deviation $s_\Delta$. With
$\tau{=}{+}2.0\times 10^{-3}$ and one-sided level $\alpha{=}0.025$:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Credit test.} One-sided paired $t$-test of
  $H_0{:}\,\mu_\Delta\le\tau$,
  $p_\tau=1-F_{t_{|S|-1}}\bigl((\bar\Delta-\tau)/(s_\Delta/\sqrt{|S|})\bigr)$.
  \item \textbf{Positivity test.} One-sided paired $t$-test of
  $H_0{:}\,\mu_\Delta\le 0$,
  $p_0=1-F_{t_{|S|-1}}\bigl(\bar\Delta/(s_\Delta/\sqrt{|S|})\bigr)$.
  \item \textbf{Adjustment.} Family~C: Holm adjustment to control
  family-wise error within the family, separately for $p_\tau$ and for
  $p_0$. Family~E: Benjamini--Hochberg adjustment for false-discovery
  rate within the family, separately for each; exploratory outcomes are
  interpreted separately from family~C. Family~D: no credit decision.
\end{itemize}

Outcomes: \textbf{Credited} if the adjusted $p_\tau\le\alpha$;
\textbf{Positive, not credited} if not credited and the adjusted
$p_0\le\alpha$; \textbf{Unsupported} otherwise. Family~E outcomes are
labelled exploratory. The count $k/|S|$ of seeds with
$\Delta_s\ge\tau$ is printed as a seed-stability descriptor and is not
a decision rule; a credited row with $k{<}|S|$ is marked seed-variable.
The one-sided $t$-tests require $|S|\ge 2$. A single-seed contrast is
reported only as a descriptive difference; it is not assigned Credited,
Positive, or Unsupported under this rule. Separately, before
multiplicity adjustment and under the same Student-$t$ model and
standard error, requiring the lower end of a two-sided 95\% interval to
reach $\tau$ is algebraically equivalent to a one-sided test at level
$0.025$; that interval rule is not used as the primary decision, and
the equivalence does not extend to decisions after Holm or
Benjamini--Hochberg adjustment.

\paragraph{Rule history}
An earlier analysis used validation AUC with a two-sided 95\% interval
bound and an every-seed threshold (the interval and seed rules;
\ref{app:ten-seed-stats}). Pooled-evaluation and archived test-only
outcomes under those rules had already been computed
(Table~\ref{tab:credit-test-10seed};
DOI \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23558062}) when the primary rule
of this section was fixed in the repository (commit \texttt{bbe4a22},
23~Oct~2026 for the written plan; commit \texttt{83d0706} for the
reanalysis tables). The primary rule is therefore not blind to those
earlier outcomes, and the credit map is reported as a reanalysis of
those runs, not as confirmation on held-out data unused to build the
rule. We use the primary rule for the decisions in this paper because
the pooled evaluation AUC plays no part in checkpoint selection and
because Holm adjustment controls family-wise error within family~C
(exploratory family~E uses FDR control separately).
Table~\ref{tab:credit-primary-changes} places the old interval-rule
label beside the new primary-rule label, with mean, LCB, and $k/10$,
for every family~C row that changes; the two ASSISTments~2012
reference-recipe packages credited under the earlier interval rule are
Positive, not credited, under the primary rule, while the other $27$
family~C rows keep the same outcome. Validation-AUC maps under the
earlier rules remain in \ref{app:ten-seed-stats} as archived
supplements, not as the decisions of this paper.

The threshold $\tau$ was fixed with the ten-seed design (Data
Availability Statement); on XES3G5M fold~0 it is seven to eight times
the seed standard deviation of the package control arms and about half
the gap from the timed tracer to the closest contextual baseline in
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}. Table~\ref{tab:credit-primary-tau}
recounts family~C outcomes at neighbouring $\tau$ under the primary
rule; Table~\ref{tab:credit-primary-mde} gives a conservative
Bonferroni first-step sensitivity calculation. Several ASSISTments
package means sit near $\tau{=}{+}2.0\times 10^{-3}$, so the operating
choice and seed variance matter for those cells.

\paragraph{Scope of the tests}
All primary tests treat the ten training seeds as replicates within one
learner fold. They quantify variability due to optimisation, not
variability over learners or corpora. Replication over the three
learner folds is reported descriptively
(Table~\ref{tab:credit-primary-var}), where the between-fold standard
deviation is smaller than the between-seed one for every contrast shown.
A learner-clustered bootstrap of the same family~C pairs
(Table~\ref{tab:learner-bootstrap}) reports sampling uncertainty over
held-out learners; it does not replace the seed-level credit test.
A credit on one encoder and a non-credit on the other is not a test
that the two effects differ.

\subsection{Registering a message}
\label{sec:protocol-register}
Algorithm~\ref{alg:register} is the interface. A new message enters
the ladder only after an availability and provenance audit, with a
declared rung, designated control, family, fixed paired seeds (ten in
this paper), and an outcome under the primary rule. A worked registration
checklist is archived with the repository (Data Availability Statement).

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Register a message on the credit ladder.}
\label{alg:register}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE \textbf{Input:} message $m$; backbone; corpus; learner fold; seeds $S$, $|S|{=}10$; $\tau{=}{+}2.0\times 10^{-3}$
\STATE Audit that every input is available at prediction time; record provenance of hint-like fields
\STATE If any input is unavailable, assign family~D and stop (diagnostic only)
\STATE Choose rung (i), (ii), or (iii), the designated control, and family C or E
\STATE If rung (iii), record the number of added parameters
\FOR{each seed $s\in S$}
  \STATE Train the on arm and the control with seed $s$ on the same fold
  \STATE $\Delta_s\gets\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{eval}}(\mathrm{on})-\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{eval}}(\mathrm{control})$
  \STATE \quad (pooled held-out evaluation AUC on original valid$\cup$test)
\ENDFOR
\STATE Compute $p_\tau$ and $p_0$; adjust within the family (Holm/FWER for C, BH/FDR for E)
\IF{adjusted $p_\tau\le 0.025$}
  \STATE outcome $\gets$ Credited
\ELSIF{adjusted $p_0\le 0.025$}
  \STATE outcome $\gets$ Positive, not credited
\ELSE
  \STATE outcome $\gets$ Unsupported
\ENDIF
\STATE Report the outcome with corpus, backbone, rung, family, and $k/|S|$; a package credit does not credit the input alone
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
