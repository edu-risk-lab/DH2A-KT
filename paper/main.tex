\begin{abstract}
Graph-augmented knowledge tracing often reports one AUC as if every
graph or time component had earned it. A real signal has to be separated
from leakage and from the parameters a new branch adds. DH$^2$A-KT is a
protocol that builds train-only auxiliary knowledge, audits hyperedge
membership leakage, and assigns each message one of three outcomes on a
three-rung ladder: capacity-matched, architecture-matched, or
package-level. A message is credited only when all five paired seeds on
XES3G5M learner fold~0 gain at least $+0.002$ validation AUC against
its designated control; a gain that is positive on every seed and still
below that cut is reported and not credited. On that fold the linear
$\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ package is credited ($5/5$; the branch adds 256
parameters) and the question--KC incidence message is not ($0/5$);
aligned gap input stays positive and below the cut ($0/5$). The
credited-minimal control reaches test-only AUC $0.8295{\pm}0.0001$.
The map is for this backbone and fold.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% =============================================================================

Knowledge tracing estimates a learner's latent mastery from interaction
history. Graph-augmented tracers then add concept edges, question--KC
incidence, or a time branch, and a paper often reports one AUC for the
whole system. That number does not say which added message earned it.
A graph built from the full log can carry held-out learners into the
test fold, and a new linear map can raise AUC without the input being
the cause.

DH$^2$A-KT is a protocol for that decision: construct train-only
auxiliary knowledge, audit membership leakage, then place each message
on a designated rung and assign one of three outcomes
(Section~\ref{sec:protocol}). The pairwise train-only construction
reused here is restated in Section~\ref{sec:p0-selfcontained}. The
LSTM arm QKC-T is the reference backbone, not the contribution.
Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal control.
Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture} is the decision map.

\subsection{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C1 (protocol).} Three rungs, three outcomes, a default
  threshold $+0.002$ inside the stability band
  $(+0.00175,+0.00236]$, a relative cut for a new corpus, and
  Algorithm~\ref{alg:register} for registering a message
  (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}).
  \item \textbf{C2 (reference map).} On XES3G5M learner fold~0 and five
  paired seeds, the linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ package is credited
  ($5/5$) and the question--KC incidence message is unsupported
  ($0/5$). Aligned gap input is consistent-positive and not credited
  ($0/5$). Table~\ref{tab:credit-decisions}.
  \item \textbf{C3 (membership diagnostic).} Path-derived concept-group
  hyperedges and a membership-leak check. The full-log positive control
  moves the leak rate from $0$ to $1$. Pairwise TBMR and $|\rho|$ do
  not separate the arms at this layer (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}).
\end{itemize}
The ladder attributes messages on a fixed backbone. It does not
decompose the gap from QKC-T to DKT ($+0.0286$), which also reflects
architecture, optimisation, and protocol. The credited package
contributes about $+0.0027$ inside the backbone.

\section{Related Work}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Graph- and hypergraph-based knowledge tracing}
Pairwise KT graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt} and dynamic
variants \cite{dygkt2024} propagate over edges; hypergraph KT
\cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and heterogeneous node types
\cite{psykt2024,hhskt2023} still draw members from KC, exercise, and
learner sets. Those papers report a model AUC. This paper asks which
message inside such a system survives a designated control. The
quantitative core is a GIKT-class LSTM with audited Q$\leftarrow$KC
incidence (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}). Hypergraph operators are
diagnostics in Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}, not five-seed
credit tests. A separate line of temporal graph memory models jointly
fits relations and forgetting and then reports one AUC; this protocol
exists to separate that architecture effect from a message effect.

\subsection{Forgetting and temporal effects in KT}
Sequence KT models have long treated elapsed time as a forgetting cue.
Nagatani et al.~\cite{nagatani2019forget} extend DKT with multiple forgetting-related
features (lag from the previous interaction and past trial counts).
HawkesKT \cite{wang2021hawkeskt} models skill-to-skill temporal
cross-effects with a Hawkes-process kernel (WSDM 2021).
Badran et al.~\cite{badran2025leakage} encode \emph{step} recency (how many KC-rows
since a concept last appeared) with learnable Fourier features; that is
not the wall-clock $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ we credit. Our P4 arm is
narrower: a per-concept exponential
$\exp(-\mathrm{softplus}(\theta_c)\cdot\mathrm{gap})$ on the same v4
backbone as Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$. That one form not meeting the gate does
not rule out power-law decay, data-driven half-lives, or Hawkes kernels;
we do not claim to have tested those alternatives.

\subsection{LLM agents and explanation faithfulness}
Closest in scope is Ni et al.~\cite{ni2026ikteia}, which aligns LLM mastery reports
with implicit DLKT states and evaluates explanation faithfulness with
KC-overlap, an independent-generation control, and paired ablations.
The Supplementary Material records an exploratory Critic-gated pilot in
that line, not a third pillar: rationales are gated over \emph{frozen}
$P(\mathrm{correct})$ with ID-anchored KC-Jaccard because XES3G5M has
no in-repo synonym KC vocabulary. Broader LLM-agent
surveys \cite{llmedu2025,agentcat2026,graphaugmentedllm2025,graphrag2025}
motivate the frozen-core split: re-inferring mastery with a multi-agent
LLM over millions of interactions is not tractable on one consumer GPU.

\subsection{Leakage-controlled evaluation}
\label{sec:related-leakage}
We searched ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, Scopus, arXiv, and Google
Scholar on 10~September~2026 with the terms ``hypergraph knowledge
tracing'', ``leakage audit'', ``graph provenance'', ``KT label leakage'',
``question-level evaluation'', and ``controlled attribution'', and
extended the same sources to leakage in graph self-supervised learning
and in recommender systems. The pairwise leakage paper~\cite{daominh2026p0}
audits pairwise prerequisite/similarity edges under train-only
construction. Within that scoped search we did not find a protocol that
applies these leakage diagnostics at the hyperedge level; this paper
adds a membership-leak diagnostic and a conceptual taxonomy
(Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}). The search log is
\texttt{docs/citation\_verification\_log.md}. We reuse P0's train-only construction,
four pairwise diagnostics, and pinned artefacts
(Section~\ref{sec:p0-selfcontained}).
Badran et al.~\cite{badran2025leakage} (arXiv:2508.17092) targets a different leakage
channel: KC-expansion \emph{label} leakage, via a MASK token in the input
embedding, plus Fourier recency (steps since a KC last appeared). That
work does not audit hyperedge provenance and does not run
capacity-matched attribution twins. Evaluation uses a
repeat-target-masked KC-expanded protocol: it masks repeat-row
\emph{targets} at scoring rather than replacing labels
in the embedding; Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ is wall-clock attempt
context, not step-count recency encoding.

Observational treatment-effect estimators in education
\cite{kaur2019causal,kumar2023causal,minn2022interpretable} are
background only.

\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}
% =============================================================================

Let $\mathcal{D} = \{(u, t, q, c, y)\}$ be a KT interaction log with learner
$u$, timestamp $t$, item $q$, knowledge component $c$, and correctness label
$y \in \{0, 1\}$, following the companion notation~\cite{daominh2026p0}. A learner-based
temporal split $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{train}},
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{val}}, \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{test}})$ assigns each
learner to exactly one fold (ratios $0.7/0.1/0.2$, three folds with split
seeds $42$/$43$/$44$, matching the companion protocol~\cite{daominh2026p0} so results
remain directly comparable, see Section~\ref{sec:reuse}).

\subsection{Self-contained summary of the reused P0 protocol}
\label{sec:p0-selfcontained}
Because the pairwise leakage manuscript~\cite{daominh2026p0} is a
separate paper, we restate the reused pieces here. That paper was
rejected by Applied Intelligence and is under review at
\emph{Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence}. This
manuscript does not wait on that decision. A preprint deposit is
prepared for the authors' arXiv account
(\texttt{docs/p0\_arxiv\_deposit.md}); no arXiv identifier is claimed
here. For each learner-based fold, $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ and
$E_{\mathrm{sim}}^f$ are built from \emph{train-fold interactions only},
then cycle-pruned to a DAG. Four pairwise diagnostics (P0 Table of
leakage metrics; computed on the train-only graph vs.\ held-out
learners) are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{flag}}$: structural learner-overlap
  indicator (whether any learner appears in more than one split);
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{overlap}}$: held-out pattern overlap on
  retained edges;
  \item $|\rho|$: magnitude of the Pearson correlation between edge
  weight and held-out outcome;
  \item $\mathrm{TBMR}$ (P0 also writes TBVR): \emph{within-train}
  temporal mixing --- the share of post-cutoff \emph{training} mass that
  supports retained prerequisite transitions --- \emph{not} a train/test
  membership violation.
\end{itemize}
P0 reports that switching from full-log to train-only graph construction
shifts baseline AUC by at most $0.003$ on XES3G5M, ASSISTments~2012, and
Junyi. We pin that repository to commit \texttt{5e3d6a0} and
copy its vendored baseline CSVs verbatim
(Section~\ref{sec:baselines-reuse}). A named-author preprint is
prepared for the authors' arXiv account
(\texttt{docs/p0\_arxiv\_deposit.md}); this manuscript does not claim
an arXiv identifier and does not attach the double-anonymized review
PDF. Chain hyperedges in this paper
are bounded-length paths on the same audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
(Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}).

We extend this notation with a hyperedge $h=(\kappa,\{(\tau_i,e_i)\},f)$:
kind $\kappa\in\{\text{path-derived concept-group},\text{session},
\text{discussion-thread},\text{teacher-intervention}\}$, typed members
(student, exercise, concept, teacher, hint, forum, discussion, video),
and fold $f$. Chain hyperedges on audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$ are audited
and used in diagnostic arms; they do \emph{not} enter QKC-T or
Zero$+\Delta t$ (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). Teacher/forum/video
kinds are interfaces; session write-backs appear only in the Tier~2
pilot. QKC-T consumes train-only Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence and
timestamps (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); Zero$+\Delta t$ keeps the
timestamps and zeros the incidence message.

\subsection{Heterogeneous hypergraph construction}
\label{sec:tier1-graph}

Path-derived concept-group hyperedges are bounded-length simple directed
paths on audited, acyclic $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (Algorithm~1). Each
hyperedge collects the concepts visited along a directed path, but
Algorithm~1 emits an \emph{unordered} member set, so path direction is
not retained in the hyperedge representation. This grouping is used for
audit and for optional HypergraphConv diagnostic arms
\cite{bai2021hypergraphconv}; it is not
part of the QKC-T / Zero$+\Delta t$ evaluation arms.

\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE \textbf{Input:} $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$; bounds $[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]=[3,8]$
\STATE \textbf{Output:} path-derived concept-group set $\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{path}}_f$
\STATE Build directed graph $G$ from $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
\FOR{each root $r$ with in-degree 0 in $G$}
  \FOR{each simple path $p$ from $r$ with $\ell_{\min}\le |p|\le\ell_{\max}$}
    \STATE emit unordered members $\{(\text{concept},c):c\in p\}$
  \ENDFOR
\ENDFOR
\end{algorithmic}
\noindent\textbf{Algorithm 1.} Path-derived concept-group hyperedges from
audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (member set is unordered; path direction is
not encoded). Chain length bounds $[3,8]$ match
\texttt{hyperedge.concept\_prerequisite} in \texttt{configs/xes3g5m.yaml}.
\label{alg:concept-hyperedge}

Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges are data-blocked on
the public KT corpora we use. Session+Hint hyperedges are built on
FoundationalASSIST only (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}). Chain
hyperedges on XES3G5M are audited and used in the graph-only diagnostic
(\ref{app:graph-only}), not as the QKC-T message-passing graph.

\subsection{Evaluation arms: LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC and time}
\label{sec:encoding}
The evaluation arm QKC-T is an LSTM backbone with train-only
question--KC incidence and a linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch; no
convolution is applied on the concept stack. We keep QKC-T for
GIKT-family parity even though the incidence \emph{message} fails the
capacity-matched gate; the credited-minimal control is Zero$+\Delta t$
(Table~\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}). (Implementation flags
\texttt{--question-graph} and \texttt{--no-graph} turn incidence on and
concept-stack \texttt{HypergraphConv} off; Table~\ref{tab:aliases}.)
Path-derived concept-group chain hyperedges are not consumed on this
path. Let $A\in\mathbb{R}^{N_q\times N_c}$
be the train-only question--KC incidence, row-normalized to $\bar{A}$.
Each item $q$ has an embedding $e_q$; each KC $c$ has $e_c$. One
propagation step, in the spirit of GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt} rather than
hypergraph convolution, is
\begin{equation}
\tilde{e}_q = \tanh\bigl(W(e_q + \bar{A}_{q,:}E_c)\bigr),
\label{eq:qkc}
\end{equation}
the implementation we run (one shared $W$ on the sum, then a separate
LSTM input projection). We do not claim a non-collapsible dual-map form.
The capacity-matched null therefore concerns this shared-sum fusion of
$e_q$ and $\bar{A}E_c$ on this backbone, not question--KC relations under
a richer gate, untied maps, or a different encoder. Attribution of the
incidence \emph{message} uses a capacity-matched twin that keeps the same
question pathway and zeros only $\bar{A}$
(\texttt{--question-incidence-control zero}); an older hard-off twin that
removed \texttt{question\_embed} and the projection pathway is
capacity-mismatched and is reported only as a pathway-off baseline
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}).
Interaction embeddings
of $(c_t,y_t)$ plus Rasch-style item parameters feed an LSTM. The next
step is scored by reading a query vector at the \emph{known} identity of
item/KC $t{+}1$ (standard next-step KT: the model is told \emph{what} will
be asked, not whether the learner will be correct).

When \texttt{--time-gap} is on, a dedicated map
$f(x)=Wx+b$ of $x=\log(1+\Delta t)$
(\texttt{nn.Linear(1, hidden)}, bias on) can be added on the LSTM input,
the next-step query, or both. A raw gap of $0$ therefore yields $f(0)=b$,
not a proven zero hidden injection.
XES3G5M's interaction timestamp is the millisecond \emph{start} of
answering; the release does not record an end time
\cite{liu2023xes3g5m}.\footnote{Official XES3G5M supplementary
documentation names the field \texttt{timestamps} and describes it as
the start time of answering in milliseconds (no end-time field). We
use that start-to-start gap throughout
Sections~\ref{sec:encoding} and~\ref{sec:threats}.}
P0 maps the raw \texttt{timestamps} column
(\texttt{configs/xes3g5m.yaml}) and stores unix seconds
(\texttt{ms}/1000) in the processed parquet
(\texttt{\_normalise\_xes\_timestamp\_ms}).
DH$^2$ sorts by $(\mathrm{user},\mathrm{timestamp},\mathrm{item})$ and
sets $\Delta t=\max(t_i-t_{i-1},0)$ seconds between consecutive rows of
the same learner, then $x=\log(1+\Delta t)$
(\texttt{log\_time\_gaps}).
A learner's first row and later KC-rows of the same attempt (identical
start time) have $\Delta t{=}0$.
Because those zeros also mark attempt boundaries, the Linear-$\Delta t$
channel can encode elapsed time \emph{and} boundary structure; we
credit the package as attempt-context timing and boundary information,
not as a pure elapsed-time effect (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
That start-to-start gap includes time on the previous question plus idle;
it is not a pure waiting time and is not the duration of the scored item.
We treat the prediction time as \emph{attempt-onset
correctness prediction}: the score is produced when the learner opens
item $t{+}1$, whose identity is already the query, not when the system
first recommends that item.
Query-side availability is therefore a deployment assumption on top of
the start-time schema, not an open question about submit vs.\ start.
LSTM-side $\Delta t$ uses only gaps between
already-observed steps (\emph{history-only}). The query-side gap is the
wait until the scored item's start; that item's identity is part of the
prediction request, so the gap is attempt context rather than a post-hoc
peek at duration after $y_{t+1}$ is observed. We do not feed the scored
step's own response into the query. We report a history-only $\Delta t$
row (\texttt{--time-gap-mode lstm}): five-seed mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00193$
($2/5$ seeds pass; not credited under the $5/5$ rule;
Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}).
Query-only: $0/5$ seeds pass (mean $+0.00099$); only \texttt{both}
meets the rule ($5/5$). A per-concept exponential forgetting form fails versus Linear on all
five seeds (P4; mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{-}0.00252$;
Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}).
HypergraphConv on multi-KC item hyperedges, hint-item hypergraphs,
dual-gated graph-only $x_t$, and the graph-sensitivity auxiliary loss
$L_{\mathrm{aux}}$ belong to the v2 diagnostic encoder
(\ref{app:graph-only}) or to diagnostic arms
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}), not to Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc}.

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:reuse}
Following the companion manuscript~\cite{daominh2026p0}, XES3G5M \cite{liu2023xes3g5m} is the
primary predictive benchmark: 18{,}066 learners, 7{,}652 items, 865~KCs
and 5{,}549{,}635 recorded attempts, which expand to 6{,}413{,}353
KC-level rows under the standard multi-KC expansion
(Section~\ref{sec:clean-protocol}).
Each interaction carries the millisecond start time of answering
\cite{liu2023xes3g5m}; P0 stores that field as unix seconds, and the
Linear $\Delta t$ branch uses start-to-start gaps
(Section~\ref{sec:encoding}).
ASSISTments~2012 GKT AUC from P0 is
not reused as a comparison column. FoundationalASSIST
\cite{foundationalassist2026} is a secondary fold with synchronized
hints (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}), not interchangeable with
classic ASSISTments~2012. Junyi supplies an expert DAG for structural
overlap only (Section~\ref{sec:junyi-gt}); we do not train the full
tracer there.

\subsection{Baselines reused from P0}
\label{sec:baselines-reuse}
KT reference baselines (DKT \cite{piech2015dkt}, AKT \cite{ghosh2020akt},
\textit{simpleKT} \cite{liu2023simplekt}, GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt},
DGEKT \cite{dgekt2024}) are trained with the vendored \textsc{pyKT}
implementations \cite{liu2022pykt} under our repeat-target-masked
chunked protocol
(Section~\ref{sec:clean-protocol}). They are a \emph{contextual}
benchmark on the same protocol, not a claim that every baseline was
tuning-matched to DH$^2$.
We reuse unpublished companion results~\cite{daominh2026p0} for BKT
\cite{corbett1995bkt}, GKT \cite{nakagawa2019gkt}, and SKT
\cite{tong2020skt}
under train-only graph construction (vendored CSVs at commit
\texttt{5e3d6a0}), except GIKT, AKT, simpleKT, DKT, and DGEKT on
XES3G5M, which we retrain under the repeat-target-masked $L{=}400$ protocol
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}).
DyGKT \cite{dygkt2024} is the nearest temporal-graph KT comparator to
the credited timing package. It was not completed under this evaluator
(the vendored pyKT stack used here does not ship a maintained DyGKT
recipe at $L{=}400$ repeat-target-masked scoring), so it is not a
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} row. That absence limits any claim that the
Linear $\Delta t$ branch is competitive with a dedicated dynamic-graph
tracer; running DyGKT under this evaluator is the first future-work
item. Inherited DKT and DGEKT are reported in

\subsection{Repeat-target-masked KC-expanded evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:clean-protocol}
Headline AUCs use a \emph{repeat-target-masked KC-expanded} protocol
aligned with \textsc{pyKT}'s repeat-format warning
\cite{liu2022pykt,liu2023simplekt}: the library's
KC-level export expands each multi-KC question into consecutive rows that
share the same timestamp and answer, so a large share of eval positions are
answerable by copying the input label. We mask repeat-row targets
(\texttt{--mask-repeats}) in both our models and retrained pyKT baselines,
use chunked windows (\texttt{--window-mode chunked}) with
$L{=}400$, and report test-only AUC on the remaining scored KC-rows.
This is \emph{not} claimed to be \textsc{pyKT}'s question-level
all-in-one evaluator \cite{liu2022pykt}: we have not fused KC scores of
the same attempt into one $p_a$. A fold-0 census finds
$208$ $L{=}400$ windows that start on a globally repeated KC-row
(all $208$ also split an attempt across the boundary;
\texttt{a3\_chunk\_boundary\_safety.json}).
Because $\mathrm{logits}[:,t]$ predicts row $t{+}1$, local index $0$ is
never a scored target; \texttt{is\_repeat} is sliced from the full-log
array, not recomputed inside the window. Invariant tests lock both
facts. Those $208$ cases are classified
\texttt{safe\_start\_not\_a\_target} with zero unknown: a chunk boundary
does not promote a repeat-row into a first target. Later KCs of the
same attempt remain masked, not pooled.
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} reports DH$^2$ arms on native scored
positions ($n{=}1{,}093{,}755$) and pyKT baselines on the library
intersection ($n{=}1{,}093{,}720$).
Credit tests and calibration use the native count.
An occurrence join on (user, dense-KC, occurrence in the scored set)
matches $1{,}093{,}718$ rows but is not a stable event ID: eight joined
keys disagree on the label (\texttt{a4\_event\_identity.json}).
The pre-chunk key
$\mathrm{user}{+}\mathrm{timestamp}{+}\mathrm{item}{+}\mathrm{KC}$
matches the same $n$ with zero label disagreement
($37$ DH$^2$-only keys, including one unmapped hashed KC; $2$ pyKT-only).
Headline five-seed gaps remain Zero$+\Delta t$ (native) minus the pyKT
row (intersection): a contextual location of the recipe, not an
event-paired difference on the common set. Rebasing every arm onto that
stable key needs stored per-row scores; those prediction files are not
in the public snapshot.
Learner-resampled CIs in
Section~\ref{sec:time-result} draw the same $3614$ learners on both
sides of one checkpoint pair; they are not event-aligned and they do not
use the occurrence join
(\ref{app:bootstrap}).
We do not collapse multi-KC rows back to attempt level for the headline
AUC: each masked KC-row target is scored once.
The residual between scoring every exported KC-row and scoring the
masked remainder is $+0.0101$ on QKC-T fold~0
(\ref{app:protocol-residual}); that is the pyKT repeat-format effect
\cite{liu2022pykt}, not an attempt-level collapse.
Published P0 numbers that score every KC row are not comparable to
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}.

\subsection{Manipulation check}
A hypergraph ablation is interpretable only if destroying the constructed
hyperedges moves AUC. Near-total node-drop $p{=}0.90$ on the graph-only
diagnostic encoder (\ref{app:graph-only}) confirms
\emph{dependence} on some graph input. Degree-preserving rewiring asks
whether that encoder also uses \emph{which} concepts co-occur: it passes
on folds~0--2 ($\Delta$AUC $0.0318$/$0.0341$/$0.0351$; shared clean
$0.7485$/$0.7511$/$0.7521$;
Table~\ref{tab:manipulation}). Relation-label permutation is near-null
on every fold (expected for a single-kind encoder). An earlier
pairwise$+$exercise path was graph-inert ($\Delta$AUC$\approx 0.0003$).
QKC-T and Zero$+\Delta t$ do not take this check: concept-stack
\texttt{HypergraphConv} is off, and Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence is a
bipartite membership matrix rather than a new multi-way graph.

\subsection{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}
Headline AUCs (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}) are repeat-target-masked $L{=}400$,
test-only. QKC-T and Zero$+\Delta t$ are both five-seed test-only
($0.8295{\pm}0.0001$); three-fold QKC-T stability is in
Table~\ref{tab:auc-p0-dt-folds}.
Training-envelope-matched GIKT remains fold~0. AKT, \textit{simpleKT},
DKT, and DGEKT inherit pyKT/P0 defaults at $L{=}400$ and are not
envelope-matched. Other baselines come from reused
P0 tables on the older budget. The GIKT edge is small (${+}0.0037$ from
Zero$+\Delta t$) and is a matched \emph{training envelope}, not a matched
tuning search (\ref{app:hparams}).
The tracer architecture was selected from a validation ablation
ladder (incidence on/off, three $\Delta t$
injection sites, and the Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}
diagnostics), whereas each baseline used a single inherited
hyperparameter vector. This search asymmetry favours the DH$^2$ arms,
not the baselines, and is a second reason the comparison is contextual.
The five-seed credit map is established on learner fold~0; repeating
paired $\Delta$ on other learner folds is future work.
Because zero gaps also mark later KC-expanded rows of the same attempt
(and learner-start rows), the Linear-$\Delta t$ channel can encode both
elapsed-time information and attempt-boundary structure
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
Learner-level
bootstrap CIs on one checkpoint are a secondary diagnostic of learner
sampling, not training-seed CIs, not event-aligned, and they do not use the
$1{,}093{,}718$-row occurrence join. HypergraphConv and
hint-item hyperedges did not meet the single-seed gate, and teacher
grouping / exponential forgetting are $0/5$, so we do not
credit multi-way message passing from those arms. The node-drop check
(\ref{app:graph-only}) is run on the graph-only diagnostic
encoder, not on QKC-T. Node-drop confirms
\emph{dependence} on some graph input. Degree-preserving rewiring on
folds~1--2 additionally moves AUC ($0.0341$/$0.0351$), which is evidence
that this diagnostic encoder is sensitive to co-occurrence structure, not
only to the presence of nodes (shared-clean AUCs in
Table~\ref{tab:manipulation}). That check still does not support
graph-structure claims about QKC-T. Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence is a
bipartite membership matrix, not a new multi-way graph.

\paragraph{Deployment availability of the query-side gap.}
Attempt-onset scoring (this paper's protocol) produces $P(y_{t+1}{=}1)$
when the learner opens item $t{+}1$, so the query-side start-to-start wait
has already elapsed. That wait still folds in time spent on the previous
question plus idle, because XES3G5M has no end timestamp
\cite{liu2023xes3g5m}. History-only / recommend-before-return uses only LSTM-side gaps
already in the log; that arm is the history-only row (five-seed mean
$\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00193$, $2/5$ seeds pass; not credited;
Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}). Serving a next item before the
learner returns is earlier still: the wait has not occurred, so
query-side $\Delta t$ must be omitted or replaced by a planned-gap proxy
we do not evaluate. The credited timing arm therefore assumes
attempt-onset availability; a history-only deployment should use the
LSTM-only arm and must not claim the both-mode $5/5$ credit
(mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00274$).

\subsection{Interpretation of the empirical findings}
\label{sec:auc-tradeoff}
The ladder answers which message survived, not whether a hypergraph
network beats GIKT. Most structural messages fail for a local reason,
not because structure is useless in every encoder.

Question--KC incidence is a bounded null under the shared-sum fusion
of Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc}: mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{=}{-}0.00010$, 95\% CI
$[{-}0.00052,{+}0.00032]$, so the mean contribution sits below about
$+0.0003$ AUC. The item pathway can still be present for architecture
parity. The incidence \emph{message} is what the gate rejects.
Richer fusion, gating, or untied maps were not run.

The linear gap is credited only as a package. The timed arm adds one
$\mathrm{Linear}(1,128)$ (256 parameters). T-zero keeps that map and
destroys the gap; T-misaligned keeps the marginal gap distribution and
breaks within-learner order. Both contrasts are positive on every seed
and below $+0.002$, which is the protocol outcome
``consistent-positive, not credited.'' History-only injection is $2/5$
and is unsupported. The credited arm therefore needs attempt-onset
gaps, and those gaps also mark KC-expansion boundaries, so the credit
is attempt-context timing and boundary information.

HypergraphConv, teacher grouping, and per-concept exponential
forgetting do not clear the gate in the configurations we ran
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Exponential forgetting loses to
the linear package on every seed (mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{-}0.00252$).
That rejects one decay form on this backbone. It does not reject
Hawkes kernels or learned half-lives. Single-seed rows (hint-item
hyperedges, a full Q-matrix, a 5{,}000-user Junyi subsample) are
diagnostics, not five-seed outcomes.

The credited messages contribute about $+0.0027$ inside the backbone.
The distance from QKC-T to DKT is $+0.0286$ and is not a share of that
package: it also reflects architecture, representations, optimisation,
and protocol. We do not convert the two distances into a percentage of
one another.

\paragraph{Question--KC incidence.}
Does not survive as credited structural knowledge under the
capacity-matched twin of Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc} ($0/5$ seeds pass;
Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}). That null is about the shared-sum fusion
on this backbone, not a claim that question--KC relations are
universally useless under a richer gate, untied maps, or another
encoder. The QKC-T evaluation arm still includes the GIKT-class
question pathway for architecture parity, but the train-only incidence
message itself is not what the gate attributes.

\paragraph{Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$.}
Survives on this backbone when both LSTM-side and query-side gaps are
present ($5/5$ vs.\ Q$\leftarrow$KC; $5/5$ vs.\ zero incidence;
Table~\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}). Those contrasts add maps as well as
gap input. T-zero / T-misaligned keep the maps: the aligned-gap
contrast is positive but below the val rule
(Table~\ref{tab:a1-timing}). History-only $\Delta t$ does not
meet the $5/5$ rule (Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}): the credited package
uses attempt-onset scoring, not a history-only forgetting cue.

\paragraph{Hypergraph convolution and path-derived concept groups.}
Did not meet the single-seed gate: \texttt{HypergraphConv} on chain
hyperedges is not credited as five-seed evidence
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Those groups remain an
audit/diagnostic artefact. The $\approx$8 point gap
versus GKT mixes encoder, budget, and protocol; it is not an isolated
architecture cost of QKC-T.

\paragraph{Hint-item hyperedges, full Q-matrix, teacher grouping, expert
DAG, exponential forgetting.}
Hint-item HE, full Q-matrix, and the Junyi DAG did not meet the
single-seed gate. Teacher grouping and per-concept exponential forgetting
are $0/5$ (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}).
Session+Hint on FoundationalASSIST ($0.720\rightarrow 0.722$) is a small
local shift, not credited as multi-way graph novelty.

The Critic pilot is exploratory and is not
part of this attribution map (Supplementary Material).

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Transfer ladders are specified and not scored in this
  submission: ASSISTments~2012 fold~0, the time package on a second
  backbone, two additional pyKT context rows, the param-padded no-time
  arm, and a fixed Junyi subsample of about 50{,}000 users. The driver
  is \texttt{scripts/50\_credit\_ladder\_plan.py}. The reference map
  above is XES3G5M fold~0 on the LSTM backbone only. If a later ladder
  disagrees, the claim narrows to ``the map depends on the corpus.''
  \item Because zero gaps also mark later KC-expanded rows of the same
  attempt (and learner-start rows), the Linear-$\Delta t$ channel can
  encode both elapsed-time information and attempt-boundary structure.
  We therefore interpret the credited package as attempt-context timing
  and boundary information rather than a pure effect of elapsed time.
  On the fold-0 test export every repeat row has raw $\Delta t{=}0$
  ($183{,}741$ rows; $14.4\%$ of unchunked P0-protocol targets, not of
  the $n{=}1{,}093{,}755$ scored set). Learner-start rows are
  $\Delta t{=}0$ by construction but are not next-step targets. A
  three-way split of true zero-second gaps between distinct attempts
  needs the processed parquet, which is not in the public snapshot. A
  no-time arm plus a $1[\Delta t{=}0]$ indicator is reserved for
  rebuttal and was not run for this submission.
  \item Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges remain
  data-blocked; session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST shift AUC
  $0.720\rightarrow 0.722$ under concept projection, but the operating-gate
  hint-item hypergraph arm does not meet the $+0.002$ gate (L in
  Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}) and is not claimed as hypergraph
  novelty.
  \item ``Does not meet the gate'' means the form is not credited in
  that configuration. In particular, the Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence
  null is for the shared $W$ fusion in Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc}; richer
  fusion, gating, or untied maps were not tested and are not ruled out.
  \item The Supplementary Material reports an IPW hint check with weak
  positivity; it is not a contribution and must not be read as a
  recommendation against hints.
  \item Teacher grouping (M4) and per-concept exponential forgetting (P4)
  do not meet the $+0.002$ gate on any seed (mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}$
  $+0.00025$ and $-0.00252$). The remaining four single-seed rows in
  Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge} (K, L, M1, M5) are diagnostics
  below the gate, not five-seed credit tests.
  \item Calibration (ECE/Brier/NLL) is reported for QKC-T, Zero$+\Delta t$,
  Q$\leftarrow$KC, and a historical pathway-off twin on the native scored
  set, and for GIKT/AKT/\textit{simpleKT} on the pyKT intersection, all
  seed~42 (Table~\ref{tab:calibration-xes3g5m}).
  We report calibration as a diagnostic, not as certification.
  \item Repeat-mask residual on QKC-T is $+0.0101$ on fold~0
  (Table~\ref{tab:protocol-residual}). Headline AUCs remain mask-target;
  we do not fuse KC scores into a question-level $p_a$.
  Gaps versus GIKT/AKT/\textit{simpleKT} stay contextual
  (distinct $n$; Section~\ref{sec:clean-protocol}).
  \item We do not evaluate a planned-gap proxy for query-side $\Delta t$
  (the wait until a recommended item is actually opened). Deployment
  that scores before the learner returns must omit query-side $\Delta t$
  (Section~\ref{sec:threats}).
  \item The hyperedge leakage audit's pairwise-projection approximation
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}) weights all projected pairs equally, so
  sampled $|\rho|$ is degenerate ($\sigma_w{=}0$), not a true near-zero.
  The full-log control moves group-membership leak to $1.0$ but not TBMR
  ($0.7341{\to}0.7357$). A hyperedge-native statistic is future work.
  \item An exploratory Critic pilot (one fold, one local 7B LLM) is in
  the Supplementary Material and is not a contribution.
  \item Reproduction environment: Windows workstation, single RTX~3090;
  unit tests pass in the released codebase.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and future work}
% =============================================================================
We presented DH$^2$A-KT as leakage-audited knowledge attribution: which
structural and temporal knowledge survives a laddered ablation with
capacity-matched, architecture-matched, and package-level controls.
The credit ladder was estimated on XES3G5M learner fold~0 using five
paired training seeds; absolute QKC-T AUC was additionally stable
across three learner folds.
The timing package passes the operating rule in package-level
ablations as attempt-context timing and boundary information.
Architecture-matched controls retain a consistently
positive aligned-gap contrast, but its mean improvement
($+0.00183$ vs.\ T-zero; $+0.00144$ vs.\ T-misaligned) remains below
the separate $+0.002$ credit threshold. Capacity-matched zeroing of
the question--KC incidence message does not meet the rule.
QKC-T is the observed-incidence evaluation arm;
Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal control. On XES3G5M
(repeat-target-masked KC-expanded, $L{=}400$, test-only), both arms
reach $0.8295{\pm}0.0001$ over five training seeds on fold~0.
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} is a contextual benchmark, not a
tuning-matched superiority claim. Three learner folds of QKC-T mean
$0.8295{\pm}0.0004$; that figure is absolute AUC, not a cross-fold
credit test. History-only $\Delta t$ is $2/5$ seeds pass and is
not credited.
Future work, in order: (i)~run DyGKT \cite{dygkt2024} under this
evaluator; (ii)~ablate backbone choices that sit outside the present
message ladder; (iii)~a question-level all-in-one evaluator;
(iv)~five-seed repeats of K/L/M1/M5; (v)~hyperedge-native leakage
statistics and the membership diagnostic on published hyperedge
formulas; (vi)~stratify results by KC density using P0's existing
group definition (prediction files already stored);
(vii)~a diagnostic permutation of KC order within multi-KC attempts
(not a replacement protocol).
