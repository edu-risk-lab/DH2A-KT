\begin{abstract}
Graph-augmented knowledge tracing (KT) often skips leakage audits of the
auxiliary knowledge it constructs, then reports a single AUC as if every
graph component contributed. We present DH$^2$A-KT, a two-tier knowledge
system that is tractable on a single consumer GPU. \textbf{Tier~1
(DH$^2$-KT)} reuses the leakage-controlled, train-only protocol of companion
paper P0, extends it with hyperedge-level diagnostics, and then
\emph{ablates which knowledge predicts}. Registered gates
($\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ a matched twin) show that bipartite
question--KC incidence and inter-step timing carry next-step accuracy,
whereas hypergraph convolution, hint-item hyperedges, a full Q-matrix,
teacher grouping, an expert DAG, and per-concept exponential forgetting
do not. On XES3G5M fold~0 under a clean protocol (mask-repeats, chunked
windows, $L{=}400$, test-only), the recommended tracer --- a v4 LSTM with
Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence and a linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch ---
reaches test AUC $0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ over five training seeds on fold~0
versus matched-budget GIKT $0.8258{\pm}0.0001$ and \textit{simpleKT}
$0.8214{\pm}0.0007$ under the same clean protocol (learner-level
bootstrap 95\% CI of each difference excludes $0$; Q$\leftarrow$KC alone
is $0.8270{\pm}0.0002$, 5/5 PASS vs.\ twin). The same recipe on folds~1--2
stays within $0.001$ (mean $0.8295{\pm}0.0004$ over learner folds). Timing is attempt context
(query at $t{+}1$), not per-concept forgetting.
\textbf{Tier~2 (AgentDG-KT)} is a Critic-gated agent layer over
\emph{frozen} Tier~1 scores. On the recommended tracer, grounded vs.\
independent-generation flag rates are $0.018$/$0.766$ with mean
ID-anchored KC-Jaccard $0.161$/$0.152$ ($n{=}500$). Dual-rater Likert
faithfulness $4.01{\pm}1.07$ / usefulness $3.52{\pm}0.71$ ($n{=}40$) was
collected on the graph-only v2 checkpoint, not on that tracer. A limited
observational hint ATE on FoundationalASSIST is reported as an
identification check with weak propensity overlap, not as a primary
claim. Graph-only v2 AUC $0.752$ and three-fold manipulation checks are a
forced-reliance diagnostic in the appendix, not the recommended tracer.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% =============================================================================

Knowledge tracing (KT) estimates a learner's latent mastery of knowledge
components (KCs) from interaction history, supporting adaptive practice,
intelligent tutoring, and curriculum recommendation. The field has
progressed from probabilistic and sequence models
\cite{piech2015dkt} to graph-augmented models that propagate information
over auxiliary concept graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt}, and, most
recently, toward hypergraph representations that capture higher-order
relations among knowledge components \cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and
dynamic, temporally-evolving graph structure \cite{dygkt2024}.

This paper builds on \cite{daominh2026p0}'s train-only leakage protocol.
Tier~1 constructs and audits graph-structured knowledge, then asks which
artefacts change next-step AUC under a registered gate. The recommended
tracer is a GIKT-family v4 LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence and a
linear $\Delta t$ branch (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); hypergraph
convolution fails that gate (Section~\ref{sec:results}). Teacher, forum,
and video members remain interfaces. Tier~2 explains frozen scores with
a Critic (Section~\ref{sec:tier2}). Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture} is the
pipeline.

\subsection{Motivation}
Three concrete gaps motivate this work:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Higher-order structure is under-audited in graph-KT.}
  Hypergraph-KT models \cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} group multiple
  knowledge components into a single hyperedge, yet published systems
  rarely audit whether those hyperedges were constructed without leakage,
  and few expose an interface for entities beyond KCs (hints, teachers,
  forums, video) when such logs become available.
  \item \textbf{Graph provenance is rarely audited.} \cite{daominh2026p0}
  shows that pooling a knowledge-component graph from the full interaction
  log before a learner-based train/test split lets held-out information
  reach a graph-augmented KT model even when the sequence optimizer itself
  trains only on the training fold. \cite{daominh2026p0}'s protocol audits
  this channel for \emph{pairwise} prerequisite/similarity edges; no
  published audit protocol currently covers \emph{hyperedges}.
  \item \textbf{Explanations of KT scores are rarely gated against a frozen
  predictor.} LLM rationales can ignore the numeric score they purport to
  explain. A knowledge system needs a leakage-closed explanation layer over
  frozen $P(\mathrm{correct})$, not a second model that re-infers mastery.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Questions}
\label{sec:rqs}
This work is motivated by a broader question: \emph{which leakage-audited
knowledge actually improves or explains next-step KT?} We decompose this
into three questions, plus entities this paper does not claim to have
learned:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RQ1} (\S\ref{sec:results}): Under a leakage audit, which
  structural knowledge improves next-step AUC?
  \item \textbf{RQ2} (\S\ref{sec:time-result}): Does temporal knowledge
  contribute as forgetting of latent mastery, or as attempt-context at
  the scored step?
  \item \textbf{RQ3} (\S\ref{sec:tier2-faithfulness-result}): Can a
  Critic-gated LLM-agent layer explain frozen Tier~1 scores without
  reopening leakage?
\end{itemize}
An observational hint ATE on FoundationalASSIST is reported as a
secondary identification check (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}),
not as a fourth research question. Interactions involving
\emph{teacher}, \emph{forum/discussion}, and \emph{video} nodes are
defined in the schema (Section~\ref{sec:prelim}) but remain data-blocked
on every corpus we use (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}): this paper
establishes an interface for those entities, not empirical evidence of
how they improve KT. A graph-only encoder and three-fold manipulation
check remain available as a forced-reliance diagnostic
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}); they are not RQ1's recommended system.

\subsection{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C1 (construction and audit).} A procedure that groups
  \cite{daominh2026p0}'s audited, acyclic prerequisite edges into
  \emph{path-derived concept-group} chain hyperedges (unordered member
  sets along directed paths; Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}) and names
  three leakage classes a pairwise audit cannot detect
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext});
  session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST
  \cite{foundationalassist2026} (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}).
  Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention types remain data-blocked
  (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Construction is not claimed as an AUC
  lift.
  \item \textbf{C2 (which knowledge predicts).} A pre-registered ablation
  (gate $\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ a matched twin) plus a
  clean-protocol tracer: Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence passes on all five
  training seeds ($5/5$ PASS); a Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch on
  that backbone passes ($5/5$ PASS; test AUC $0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ on
  XES3G5M fold~0, mean $0.8295{\pm}0.0004$ across three learner folds).
  HypergraphConv, hint-item hyperedges, a full Q-matrix, teacher
  grouping, an expert DAG, and per-concept forgetting fail
  (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Versus matched-budget GIKT
  $0.8258{\pm}0.0001$, the recommended tracer's learner-level bootstrap
  95\% CI excludes $0$ (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). Graph-only v2 mean AUC
  $0.752$ versus GKT $0.834$ is an appendix diagnostic, not the
  recommended tracer.
  \item \textbf{C3 (Critic-gated explanations).} A Tier~2 pilot
  (AgentDG-KT, Section~\ref{sec:tier2}) over frozen Tier~1 scores at
  $n{=}500$ on a consumer GPU. On the recommended tracer, grounded vs.\
  independent-generation flag rates are $0.018$/$0.766$ (mean JC
  $0.161$/$0.152$). Dual-rater Likert ($n{=}40$) is reported on the v2
  checkpoint (Table~\ref{tab:human-eval}). Comparison with
  \cite{ni2026ikteia} is a probability gate, not synonym-aware KC overlap
  or an AUC contest --- pilot evidence, not a classroom RCT.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Graph- and hypergraph-based knowledge tracing}
Pairwise KT graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt} and dynamic
variants \cite{dygkt2024} propagate over edges; hypergraph KT
\cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and heterogeneous node types
\cite{psykt2024,hhskt2023} still draw members from KC/exercise/learner
sets. None treat Teacher, Hint, Forum, or Video as first-class hyperedge
members with KCs --- C1's \emph{interface}. This paper's quantitative
core is a GIKT-class LSTM with audited Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence
(Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); hypergraph operators are registered
failures (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}).

\subsection{LLM agents and explanation faithfulness}
Closest in scope is \cite{ni2026ikteia}, which aligns LLM mastery reports
with implicit DLKT states and evaluates explanation faithfulness with
KC-overlap, an independent-generation control, and paired ablations.
AgentDG-KT (Section~\ref{sec:tier2}) complements that line: we audit the
auxiliary graph before explanation, gate rationales with a Critic over
\emph{frozen} $P(\mathrm{correct})$, and use ID-anchored KC-Jaccard
because XES3G5M has no in-repo synonym KC vocabulary. Broader LLM-agent
surveys \cite{llmedu2025,agentcat2026,graphaugmentedllm2025,graphrag2025}
motivate the frozen-core split: re-inferring mastery with a multi-agent
LLM over millions of interactions is not tractable on one consumer GPU.

\subsection{Leakage-controlled evaluation}
\label{sec:related-leakage}
\cite{daominh2026p0} (companion P0, under review) shows that pooling a
KC graph from the full log before a learner split leaks held-out
information through the graph even if the sequence optimizer trains only
on the training fold. We reuse its train-only construction, four
pairwise diagnostics, and pinned artefacts
(Section~\ref{sec:p0-selfcontained}). This paper extends the audit from
pairwise edges to hyperedges (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}).

Observational treatment-effect estimators in education
\cite{kaur2019causal,kumar2023causal,minn2022interpretable} are
background only: our IPW hint ATE is a secondary identification check,
not a primary contribution (Section~\ref{sec:causal}).

\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}
% =============================================================================

Let $\mathcal{D} = \{(u, t, q, c, y)\}$ be a KT interaction log with learner
$u$, timestamp $t$, item $q$, knowledge component $c$, and correctness label
$y \in \{0, 1\}$, following \cite{daominh2026p0}'s notation. A learner-based
temporal split $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{train}},
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{val}}, \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{test}})$ assigns each
learner to exactly one fold (ratios $0.7/0.1/0.2$, three folds with split
seeds $42$/$43$/$44$, matching \cite{daominh2026p0}'s protocol so results
remain directly comparable, see Section~\ref{sec:reuse}).

\subsection{Self-contained summary of the reused P0 protocol}
\label{sec:p0-selfcontained}
Because \cite{daominh2026p0} is under review, we state the reused pieces
here. For each learner-based fold, $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ and
$E_{\mathrm{sim}}^f$ are built from \emph{train-fold interactions only},
then cycle-pruned to a DAG. Four pairwise diagnostics (P0 Table of
leakage metrics; computed on the train-only graph vs.\ held-out
learners) are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{flag}}$: structural learner-overlap
  indicator (whether any learner appears in more than one split);
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{overlap}}$: held-out pattern overlap on
  retained edges;
  \item $|\rho|$: magnitude of the Pearson correlation between edge
  weight and held-out outcome;
  \item $\mathrm{TBMR}$ (P0 also writes TBVR): \emph{within-train}
  temporal mixing --- the share of post-cutoff \emph{training} mass that
  supports retained prerequisite transitions --- \emph{not} a train/test
  membership violation.
\end{itemize}
P0 reports that switching from full-log to train-only graph construction
shifts baseline AUC by at most $0.003$ on XES3G5M, ASSISTments~2012, and
Junyi. We pin the companion repository to commit \texttt{5e3d6a0} and
copy its published baseline CSVs verbatim
(Section~\ref{sec:baselines-reuse}). The companion PDF can be attached as
journal supplementary material on request. Chain hyperedges in this paper
are bounded-length paths on the same audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
(Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}).

We extend this notation with a hyperedge $h=(\kappa,\{(\tau_i,e_i)\},f)$:
kind $\kappa\in\{\text{path-derived concept-group},\text{session},
\text{discussion-thread},\text{teacher-intervention}\}$, typed members
(student, exercise, concept, teacher, hint, forum, discussion, video),
and fold $f$. Chain hyperedges on audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$ are audited
and used in FAIL/diagnostic arms; they do \emph{not} enter the
recommended tracer (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). Teacher/forum/video
kinds are interfaces; session write-backs appear only in the Tier~2
pilot. The recommended tracer consumes train-only Q$\leftarrow$KC
incidence and timestamps inside a v4 LSTM (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}).

\subsection{Heterogeneous hypergraph construction}
\label{sec:tier1-graph}

Concept-prerequisite hyperedges are bounded-length simple directed paths
on audited, acyclic $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (Algorithm~1). We call the
resulting hyperedges \emph{path-derived concept-group} hyperedges: each
hyperedge collects the concepts visited along a directed path in
$E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$, but Algorithm~1 emits an \emph{unordered} member
set, so prerequisite direction is not retained in the hyperedge
representation. This grouping is used for audit and for optional
\texttt{HypergraphConv} diagnostic arms; it is not prerequisite-preserving
message passing and is not part of the recommended Q$\leftarrow$KC tracer.

\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE \textbf{Input:} $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$; bounds $[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]$
\STATE \textbf{Output:} path-derived concept-group set $\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{path}}_f$
\STATE Build directed graph $G$ from $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
\FOR{each root $r$ with in-degree 0 in $G$}
  \FOR{each simple path $p$ from $r$ with $\ell_{\min}\le |p|\le\ell_{\max}$}
    \STATE emit unordered members $\{(\text{concept},c):c\in p\}$
  \ENDFOR
\ENDFOR
\end{algorithmic}
\noindent\textbf{Algorithm 1.} Path-derived concept-group hyperedges from
audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (member set is unordered; path direction is
not encoded).
\label{alg:concept-hyperedge}

Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges are data-blocked on
the public KT corpora we use. Session+Hint hyperedges are built on
FoundationalASSIST only (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}). Chain
hyperedges on XES3G5M are audited and used in the graph-only diagnostic
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}), not as the recommended tracer's
message-passing graph.

\subsection{Recommended tracer: v4 LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC and time}
\label{sec:encoding}
The recommended DH$^2$-KT tracer is architecture v4 with
\texttt{--question-graph} and \texttt{--no-graph} on the concept stack:
path-derived concept-group chain hyperedges are \emph{not} consumed by
\texttt{HypergraphConv} on this path. Let $A\in\mathbb{R}^{N_q\times N_c}$
be the train-only question--KC incidence, row-normalized to $\bar{A}$.
Each item $q$ has an embedding $e_q$; each KC $c$ has $e_c$. One
propagation step, in the spirit of GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt} rather than
hypergraph convolution, is
\begin{equation}
\tilde{e}_q = \tanh\bigl(W(e_q + \bar{A}_{q,:}\,E_c)\bigr),
\label{eq:qkc}
\end{equation}
with a dedicated linear $W$ so the mix cannot collapse into $e_c$
(registered twin: same LSTM without this branch). Interaction embeddings
of $(c_t,y_t)$ plus Rasch-style item parameters feed an LSTM. The next
step is scored by reading a query vector at the \emph{known} identity of
item/KC $t{+}1$ (standard next-step KT: the model is told \emph{what} will
be asked, not whether the learner will be correct).

When \texttt{--time-gap} is on (mode \texttt{both} on XES3G5M), a
dedicated linear map of $\log(1+\Delta t)$ is added on the LSTM input and
on that query, where $\Delta t$ is the inter-step gap aligned with the
row being encoded. The query-side gap is the wait until the scored item;
that item's identity is part of the prediction request, so the gap is
attempt context rather than a post-hoc peek at duration after $y_{t+1}$
is observed. We do not feed the scored step's own response into the
query. Per-concept exponential forgetting is a registered alternative
that fails the gate (P4). HypergraphConv on multi-KC item hyperedges,
hint-item hypergraphs, dual-gated graph-only $x_t$, and the
graph-sensitivity auxiliary loss $L_{\mathrm{aux}}$ belong to the v2
diagnostic encoder (Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}) or to FAIL arms
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}), not to Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc}.

\subsection{Observational ATE (secondary)}
\label{sec:causal}
On FoundationalASSIST we report a standard inverse-propensity (IPW)
average treatment effect of $\texttt{hint\_used}_t$ on
$\texttt{correct}_{t+1}$, with logistic propensity on pre-$t$ summaries
and clipping documented in Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}.
Unconfoundedness is assumed, not proven. Same-step
\texttt{discrete\_score} is not the outcome (it is mechanically tied to
hint requests). No ATE is reported on XES3G5M (no hint logs). This check
is not a primary contribution.

\section{Tier 2: AgentDG-KT pilot}
\label{sec:tier2}
% =============================================================================

Tier~2 is deliberately scoped as a pilot, not a benchmark at Tier~1's
quantitative rigor: a full multi-agent LLM pipeline that re-infers
knowledge state over millions of interactions is not tractable on a
single consumer GPU within a realistic project timeline. Three agents
operate on Tier~1's \emph{frozen} output rather than re-inferring
knowledge state:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Diagnostician Agent} produces a natural-language rationale
  for a given Tier-1 prediction, explicitly instructed not to override the
  given probability.
  \item \textbf{Tutor/Hint Agent} proposes the next hint/exercise and writes
  a new session hyperedge back into the graph (the agentic closed loop).
  \item \textbf{Critic Agent} is a \emph{mandatory} quality gate that flags
  any Diagnostician output contradicting or ignoring the Tier-1 number it
  was meant to explain, before anything is treated as ground truth in a
  case study.
\end{itemize}

We ran a 500-sample pilot on XES3G5M fold~0 (seed 42, Qwen2.5-7B via
Ollama) on the graph-only v2 checkpoint and re-ran the same prompt on
the recommended tracer (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}). The Diagnostician
emits a \texttt{GroundedKCs} trailer for ID-anchored KC-Jaccard; IG omits
frozen $P(\mathrm{correct})$ from that prompt while the Critic still sees
the true score. Flag rates and JC are in
Section~\ref{sec:tier2-faithfulness-result}. The Tutor wrote 500 session
hyperedges at pilot scale.

\section{Experimental setup}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:reuse}
Following \cite{daominh2026p0}, XES3G5M \cite{liu2023xes3g5m}
(18{,}066 learners, 7{,}652 items, 865 KCs, 6{,}413{,}353 interactions)
is the primary predictive benchmark. ASSISTments~2012 GKT AUC from P0 is
not reused as a comparison column. FoundationalASSIST
\cite{foundationalassist2026} is a secondary fold with synchronized
hints (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}), not interchangeable with
classic ASSISTments~2012. Junyi supplies an expert DAG for structural
overlap only (Section~\ref{sec:junyi-gt}); we do not train the full
tracer there.

\subsection{Baselines reused from P0}
\label{sec:baselines-reuse}
Sequence baselines (DKT, AKT, simpleKT, GIKT, DyGKT, DGEKT) are trained
with the vendored \textsc{pyKT} implementations \cite{liu2022pykt} under
our clean chunked protocol (Section~\ref{sec:clean-protocol}); we treat them as
fair comparisons, not as novel KT architectures discovered here
\cite{liu2023simplekt,ghosh2020akt,yang2021gikt,dygkt2024,hgkt2025}.
We reuse \cite{daominh2026p0}'s published results for BKT, GKT, and SKT
under train-only graph construction (vendored CSVs at commit
\texttt{5e3d6a0}), except GIKT, AKT, simpleKT, DKT, DyGKT, and DGEKT on
XES3G5M, which we retrain under the clean $L{=}400$ protocol
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). A config-checksum script confirms the
XES3G5M P0 config match. The companion PDF is available to reviewers as
supplementary material on request.

\subsection{Clean evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:clean-protocol}
Headline AUCs use a \emph{clean} protocol aligned with \textsc{pyKT}'s
repeat-format warning \cite{liu2022pykt,liu2023simplekt}: the library's
KC-level export expands each multi-KC question into consecutive rows that
share the same timestamp and answer, so a large share of eval positions are
answerable by copying the input label. We therefore mask repeat-row targets
(\texttt{--mask-repeats}) in both our models and retrained pyKT baselines,
use chunked windows (\texttt{--window-mode chunked}) with
$L{=}400$, and report test-only AUC on the remaining scored positions
($n_{\mathrm{scored}}\approx 1.09\times 10^6$ on XES3G5M fold~0).
Published P0 numbers that score every KC row are not comparable to this
table. Our contribution is a fair retrain under the shared masking rules,
not the discovery of KC-level row repeats.

\subsection{Manipulation check}
A hypergraph ablation is interpretable only if destroying the constructed
hyperedges moves AUC (near-total node-drop $p{=}0.90$). The graph-only v2
encoder passes on all three XES3G5M folds
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}); an earlier pairwise$+$exercise path was
graph-inert ($\Delta$AUC$\approx 0.0003$). The recommended v4 tracer uses
\texttt{--no-graph} and is not claimed to pass this destruction check.

\subsection{Which structural knowledge predicts (RQ1)}

\subsection{Tier~2 faithfulness pilot (RQ3)}
\label{sec:tier2-faithfulness-result}
See Table~\ref{tab:tier2} and Section~\ref{sec:tier2}. The Tutor wrote 500
session hyperedges at pilot scale. Following \cite{ni2026ikteia}'s use of a
corpus-wide KC-overlap faithfulness metric and an independent-generation
negative control, we additionally compute \emph{ID-anchored KC-Jaccard}
over Diagnostician outputs: the model must emit a structured
\texttt{GroundedKCs: $[\ldots]$} trailer; support is the target concept,
KCs in the recent history window, and their 1-hop neighbors on audited
train-only $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$. The IG arm uses the same history but omits
frozen $P(\mathrm{correct})$ from the Diagnostician prompt while the Critic
still sees the true Tier~1 score (Table~\ref{tab:kc-jaccard-ig}). On
Qwen2.5-7B (Ollama, $n{=}500$), grounded explanations have flag rate

gate mainly catches probability-ungrounded rationales rather than
rewriting KC overlap. The same prompt on the recommended

pattern: grounded/IG flag $0.018$/$0.766$, mean JC $0.161$/$0.152$.

pipeline (flag $0.0$ vs.\ $1.0$ at matched JC). From the calibrated
Ollama v2 run we drew a fixed 40-sample pack (seed 42) for two

Pooled means are $4.01{\pm}1.07$ (faithfulness) and $3.52{\pm}0.71$
(usefulness); pairwise Spearman $\rho{=}0.71$ / $0.58$ and within-$\pm$1
rates of 92.5\% / 97.5\% (Table~\ref{tab:human-eval}). A third rater was
optional and not required for this pilot report.

\subsection{Secondary corpus: FoundationalASSIST}
\label{sec:foundational-assist}
FoundationalASSIST \cite{foundationalassist2026} provides
$\approx$1.74M skill-linked interactions with synchronized

learner-temporal protocol as P0 (0.7/0.1/0.2, fold~0), we infer
train-only $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$ (280 edges after cycle pruning), build
5{,}840 path-derived concept-group chain hyperedges, and construct

DH$^2$-KT fold~0 AUC is 0.720 with path-derived concept-group hyperedges alone

\section{Discussion}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Threats to validity}
Headline AUCs (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}) are clean $L{=}400$,
test-only. Q$\leftarrow$KC$+\Delta t$ is also on folds~1--2;
matched-budget GIKT/AKT remain fold~0. Other baselines come from reused
P0 tables on the older budget. The GIKT edge is small; we report
learner-level bootstrap CIs, not a SOTA headline. HypergraphConv and
hint-item hyperedges fail the registered gate, so we do not credit
multi-way message passing. The v2 destruction check
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}) shows that \emph{a} backbone can be
forced to use a hypergraph; it does not identify which row of
Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge} drives the recommended tracer.

\subsection{What KBS should take from a small AUC edge}
\label{sec:auc-tradeoff}
DH$^2$-KT is a leakage-audited knowledge system, not ``hypergraph NNs
beat GIKT'': (i)~hyperedge diagnostics beyond pairwise $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$;
(ii)~a registered map of which knowledge predicts; (iii)~a Critic gate
over frozen $P(\mathrm{correct})$, vs.\ \cite{ni2026ikteia} as a
probability gate rather than synonym-aware KC overlap. The $\approx$8
point gap versus GKT is the cost of the graph-only diagnostic, not of
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}. The hint ATE is a weak-overlap check, not a
fourth pillar. Dual-rater Likert ($n{=}40$) is on v2; automatic flag/JC
in the abstract use the recommended tracer.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges remain
  data-blocked; session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST shift AUC
  $0.720\rightarrow 0.722$ under concept projection, but the registered
  hint-item hypergraph arm fails the $+0.002$ gate (L in
  Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}) and is not claimed as hypergraph
  novelty.
  \item The causal-effect layer's identification assumption (no unmeasured
  confounding given observed features) is stated, not proven; the
  FoundationalASSIST hint ATE shows weak propensity overlap and should not
  be read as a recommendation against hints.
  \item The hyperedge leakage audit's pairwise-projection approximation
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}) weights all projected pairs equally; a
  hyperedge-native statistic is future work.
  \item Tier~2 pilot: one fold, one local 7B LLM; human ratings use $n{=}40$
  and two raters on the \emph{v2} graph-only checkpoint (no third rater;
  not a classroom deployment study; not the recommended tracer).
  Automatic flag/JC numbers in the abstract use the
  Q$\leftarrow$KC$+\Delta t$ re-run. ID-anchored KC-Jaccard is not
  synonym-aware (XES3G5M lacks an in-repo KC name vocabulary); the
  grounded--IG $\Delta$JC is small ($\approx{+}0.010$), so KC-overlap
  alone is a weak separator relative to Critic flag rate. An LLM-scale
  sensitivity sweep remains optional future work.
  \item Reproduction environment: Windows workstation, single RTX~3090;
  unit tests pass in the released codebase.
\end{itemize}

% =============================================================================
\section{Conclusion and future work}
% =============================================================================
We presented DH$^2$A-KT: leakage-audited construction, a registered map
of which knowledge predicts (Q$\leftarrow$KC and Linear $\Delta t$ live;
hypergraph convolution and several metadata sources fail), and a
Critic-gated explanation layer over frozen scores. On XES3G5M (clean
$L{=}400$, test-only), the recommended tracer reaches
$0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ over five training seeds on fold~0 versus
matched-budget GIKT $0.8258{\pm}0.0001$ ($0.8295{\pm}0.0004$ over three
learner folds). Timing is attempt context, not concept-forget. Tier~2 grounded/IG
flag rates on that tracer are $0.018$/$0.766$. Future work: matched
GIKT/AKT on folds~1--2, human ratings on the recommended tracer,
hyperedge-native leakage statistics, and richer ATE confounders.
