\begin{abstract}
Graph-augmented knowledge tracing (KT) often constructs auxiliary
knowledge without a leakage audit, then reports a single AUC as if every
graph component contributed. This paper asks a leakage-audited
attribution question: which structural and temporal knowledge survives a
controlled ablation? DH$^2$A-KT is an attribution pipeline that is
tractable on a single consumer GPU (the $^2$ marks heterogeneous
hypergraph \emph{interfaces}; the recommended tracer does not use
hypergraph convolution). It reuses the train-only protocol of companion
paper P0, extends the audit to hyperedges, and then ablates which
knowledge predicts. Pre-specified gates
($\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ a matched twin) show that Linear
$\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ carries next-step accuracy on this backbone, whereas a
capacity-matched zeroing of the train-only question--KC incidence
\emph{message} fails ($0/5$ PASS; mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{\approx}{-}0.0001$),
as do hypergraph convolution, hint-item hyperedges, a full Q-matrix,
teacher grouping, an expert DAG, and per-concept exponential forgetting.
An older hard-off twin that removed the whole question pathway is
capacity-mismatched and is not used to credit incidence. On XES3G5M
fold~0 under a clean protocol (mask-repeats, chunked windows, $L{=}400$,
test-only), the recommended tracer (QKC-T: LSTM with the Q$\leftarrow$KC
pathway and a linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch) reaches test AUC
$0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ over five training seeds versus compute-matched GIKT
$0.8258{\pm}0.0001$ and \textit{simpleKT} $0.8214{\pm}0.0007$
(learner-level bootstrap 95\% CI of each difference excludes $0$). The
same recipe on folds~1--2 stays within $0.001$ (mean
$0.8295{\pm}0.0004$). On this backbone the exponential forgetting form
(P4) does not outperform Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$; LSTM-only $\Delta t$
fails the gate. A graph-only diagnostic encoder (AUC $0.752$) is in the
appendix, not the recommended tracer.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% =============================================================================

Knowledge tracing (KT) estimates a learner's latent mastery of knowledge
components (KCs) from interaction history, supporting adaptive practice,
intelligent tutoring, and curriculum recommendation. The field has
progressed from probabilistic and sequence models
\cite{piech2015dkt} to graph-augmented models that propagate information
over auxiliary concept graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt}, and, most
recently, toward hypergraph representations that capture higher-order
relations among knowledge components \cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and
dynamic, temporally-evolving graph structure \cite{dygkt2024}.

This paper builds on \cite{daominh2026p0}'s train-only leakage protocol.
DH$^2$A-KT is a leakage-audited \emph{attribution} setting: construct,
audit, then ask which knowledge predicts. The recommended
tracer (QKC-T) is a GIKT-family LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence and a
linear $\Delta t$ branch (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); hypergraph
convolution fails that gate (Section~\ref{sec:results}). Teacher, forum,
and video members remain interfaces. Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture} is the
pipeline.

\subsection{Motivation}
Three concrete gaps motivate this work:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Higher-order structure is under-audited in graph-KT.}
  Hypergraph-KT models \cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} group multiple
  knowledge components into a single hyperedge, yet published systems
  rarely audit whether those hyperedges were constructed without leakage,
  and few expose an interface for entities beyond KCs (hints, teachers,
  forums, video) when such logs become available.
  \item \textbf{Graph provenance is rarely audited.} \cite{daominh2026p0}
  shows that pooling a knowledge-component graph from the full interaction
  log before a learner-based train/test split lets held-out information
  reach a graph-augmented KT model even when the sequence optimizer itself
  trains only on the training fold. \cite{daominh2026p0}'s protocol audits
  this channel for \emph{pairwise} prerequisite/similarity edges; published
  hyperedge-level audits remain scarce.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Questions}
\label{sec:rqs}
This work is motivated by a broader question: \emph{which leakage-audited
knowledge actually improves next-step KT?} We decompose this into two
questions, plus entities this paper does not claim to have learned:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RQ1} (\S\ref{sec:results}): Under a leakage audit, which
  structural knowledge improves next-step AUC?
  \item \textbf{RQ2} (\S\ref{sec:time-result}): On this backbone, does a
  Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch outperform the per-concept exponential
  forgetting form we test, and is the surviving timing gain available as
  LSTM-side attempt context?
\end{itemize}
An exploratory Critic-gated survey over frozen scores is reported after
the predictive results (Section~\ref{sec:tier2}); it is not a third
research question and not a contribution.
Teacher, forum/discussion, and video nodes are
defined in the schema (Section~\ref{sec:prelim}) but remain data-blocked
on every corpus we use (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}): this paper
establishes an interface for those entities, not empirical evidence of
how they improve KT. A graph-only encoder and three-fold manipulation
check remain available as a forced-reliance diagnostic
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}); they are not RQ1's recommended system.

\subsection{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C1 (construction and audit).} A procedure that groups
  \cite{daominh2026p0}'s audited, acyclic prerequisite edges into
  \emph{path-derived concept-group} chain hyperedges (unordered member
  sets along directed paths; Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}) and names
  three leakage classes that a pairwise projection does not express
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext});
  session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST
  \cite{foundationalassist2026} (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}).
  Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention types remain data-blocked
  (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Construction is not claimed as an AUC
  lift.
  \item \textbf{C2 (which knowledge predicts).} A pre-specified ablation
  (gate $\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ a matched twin; an internal
  convention locked before the ladder, not a public registry) plus a
  clean-protocol tracer: Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ on the QKC-T backbone
  passes ($5/5$ PASS; test AUC $0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ on XES3G5M fold~0, mean
  $0.8295{\pm}0.0004$ across three learner folds). A capacity-matched
  zero-incidence twin fails ($0/5$ PASS; Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}), so
  the incidence \emph{message} is not credited. HypergraphConv, hint-item
  hyperedges, a full Q-matrix, teacher grouping, an expert DAG, and
  per-concept exponential forgetting (P4) also fail
  (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Versus compute-matched GIKT
  $0.8258{\pm}0.0001$, the recommended tracer's learner-level bootstrap
  95\% CI excludes $0$ (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). Graph-only v2 mean AUC
  $0.752$ versus GKT $0.834$ is an appendix diagnostic, not the
  recommended tracer.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Graph- and hypergraph-based knowledge tracing}
Pairwise KT graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt} and dynamic
variants \cite{dygkt2024} propagate over edges; hypergraph KT
\cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and heterogeneous node types
\cite{psykt2024,hhskt2023} still draw members from KC/exercise/learner
sets. Among the hypergraph-KT systems we read
\cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025,psykt2024,hhskt2023}, Teacher, Hint,
Forum, and Video appear as interfaces here rather than as evaluated
members. This paper's quantitative core is a GIKT-class LSTM with audited
Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); hypergraph
operators are pre-specified failures (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}).

\subsection{Forgetting and temporal effects in KT}
Sequence KT models have long treated elapsed time as a forgetting cue.
\cite{nagatani2019forget} extend DKT with multiple forgetting-related
features (lag from the previous interaction and past trial counts).
HawkesKT \cite{wang2021hawkeskt} models skill-to-skill temporal
cross-effects with a Hawkes-process kernel (WSDM 2021). Our P4 arm is
narrower: a per-concept exponential
$\exp(-\mathrm{softplus}(\theta_c)\cdot\mathrm{gap})$ on the same v4
backbone as Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$. A FAIL of that one form does
not rule out power-law decay, data-driven half-lives, or Hawkes kernels;
we do not claim to have tested those alternatives.

\subsection{LLM agents and explanation faithfulness}
Closest in scope is \cite{ni2026ikteia}, which aligns LLM mastery reports
with implicit DLKT states and evaluates explanation faithfulness with
KC-overlap, an independent-generation control, and paired ablations.
Section~\ref{sec:tier2} is a scoped survey in that line, not a third
pillar: we gate rationales with a Critic over \emph{frozen}
$P(\mathrm{correct})$ and use ID-anchored KC-Jaccard because XES3G5M has
no in-repo synonym KC vocabulary. Broader LLM-agent
surveys \cite{llmedu2025,agentcat2026,graphaugmentedllm2025,graphrag2025}
motivate the frozen-core split: re-inferring mastery with a multi-agent
LLM over millions of interactions is not tractable on one consumer GPU.

\subsection{Leakage-controlled evaluation}
\label{sec:related-leakage}
We searched ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and Scopus through July 2026
with the terms ``hypergraph knowledge tracing'', ``leakage audit'', and
``graph provenance''. \cite{daominh2026p0} (companion P0, under review)
audits pairwise prerequisite/similarity edges under train-only
construction. Within that search we found no published protocol that
applies the same leakage diagnostics to \emph{hyperedges}; this paper
extends the audit from pairwise edges to hyperedges
(Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}). We reuse P0's train-only construction,
four pairwise diagnostics, and pinned artefacts
(Section~\ref{sec:p0-selfcontained}).

Observational treatment-effect estimators in education
\cite{kaur2019causal,kumar2023causal,minn2022interpretable} are
background only.

\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}
% =============================================================================

Let $\mathcal{D} = \{(u, t, q, c, y)\}$ be a KT interaction log with learner
$u$, timestamp $t$, item $q$, knowledge component $c$, and correctness label
$y \in \{0, 1\}$, following \cite{daominh2026p0}'s notation. A learner-based
temporal split $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{train}},
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{val}}, \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{test}})$ assigns each
learner to exactly one fold (ratios $0.7/0.1/0.2$, three folds with split
seeds $42$/$43$/$44$, matching \cite{daominh2026p0}'s protocol so results
remain directly comparable, see Section~\ref{sec:reuse}).

\subsection{Self-contained summary of the reused P0 protocol}
\label{sec:p0-selfcontained}
Because \cite{daominh2026p0} is under review, we state the reused pieces
here. For each learner-based fold, $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ and
$E_{\mathrm{sim}}^f$ are built from \emph{train-fold interactions only},
then cycle-pruned to a DAG. Four pairwise diagnostics (P0 Table of
leakage metrics; computed on the train-only graph vs.\ held-out
learners) are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{flag}}$: structural learner-overlap
  indicator (whether any learner appears in more than one split);
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{overlap}}$: held-out pattern overlap on
  retained edges;
  \item $|\rho|$: magnitude of the Pearson correlation between edge
  weight and held-out outcome;
  \item $\mathrm{TBMR}$ (P0 also writes TBVR): \emph{within-train}
  temporal mixing --- the share of post-cutoff \emph{training} mass that
  supports retained prerequisite transitions --- \emph{not} a train/test
  membership violation.
\end{itemize}
P0 reports that switching from full-log to train-only graph construction
shifts baseline AUC by at most $0.003$ on XES3G5M, ASSISTments~2012, and
Junyi. We pin the companion repository to commit \texttt{5e3d6a0} and
copy its published baseline CSVs verbatim
(Section~\ref{sec:baselines-reuse}). The companion PDF can be attached as
journal supplementary material on request. Chain hyperedges in this paper
are bounded-length paths on the same audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
(Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}).

We extend this notation with a hyperedge $h=(\kappa,\{(\tau_i,e_i)\},f)$:
kind $\kappa\in\{\text{path-derived concept-group},\text{session},
\text{discussion-thread},\text{teacher-intervention}\}$, typed members
(student, exercise, concept, teacher, hint, forum, discussion, video),
and fold $f$. Chain hyperedges on audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$ are audited
and used in FAIL/diagnostic arms; they do \emph{not} enter the
recommended tracer (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). Teacher/forum/video
kinds are interfaces; session write-backs appear only in the Tier~2
pilot. The recommended tracer consumes train-only Q$\leftarrow$KC
incidence and timestamps inside the recommended tracer
(Section~\ref{sec:encoding}).

\subsection{Heterogeneous hypergraph construction}
\label{sec:tier1-graph}

Path-derived concept-group hyperedges are bounded-length simple directed
paths on audited, acyclic $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (Algorithm~1). Each
hyperedge collects the concepts visited along a directed path, but
Algorithm~1 emits an \emph{unordered} member set, so path direction is
not retained in the hyperedge representation. This grouping is used for
audit and for optional \texttt{HypergraphConv} diagnostic arms; it is not
part of the recommended Q$\leftarrow$KC tracer.

\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE \textbf{Input:} $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$; bounds $[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]=[3,8]$
\STATE \textbf{Output:} path-derived concept-group set $\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{path}}_f$
\STATE Build directed graph $G$ from $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
\FOR{each root $r$ with in-degree 0 in $G$}
  \FOR{each simple path $p$ from $r$ with $\ell_{\min}\le |p|\le\ell_{\max}$}
    \STATE emit unordered members $\{(\text{concept},c):c\in p\}$
  \ENDFOR
\ENDFOR
\end{algorithmic}
\noindent\textbf{Algorithm 1.} Path-derived concept-group hyperedges from
audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (member set is unordered; path direction is
not encoded). Chain length bounds $[3,8]$ match
\texttt{hyperedge.concept\_prerequisite} in \texttt{configs/xes3g5m.yaml}.
\label{alg:concept-hyperedge}

Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges are data-blocked on
the public KT corpora we use. Session+Hint hyperedges are built on
FoundationalASSIST only (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}). Chain
hyperedges on XES3G5M are audited and used in the graph-only diagnostic
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}), not as the recommended tracer's
message-passing graph.

\subsection{Recommended tracer: LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC and time}
\label{sec:encoding}
The recommended tracer, denoted QKC-T, is an LSTM backbone with train-only
question--KC incidence and a linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch; no
convolution is applied on the concept stack. (Implementation flags
\texttt{--question-graph} and \texttt{--no-graph} turn incidence on and
concept-stack \texttt{HypergraphConv} off; Table~\ref{tab:aliases}.)
Path-derived concept-group chain hyperedges are not consumed on this
path. Let $A\in\mathbb{R}^{N_q\times N_c}$
be the train-only question--KC incidence, row-normalized to $\bar{A}$.
Each item $q$ has an embedding $e_q$; each KC $c$ has $e_c$. One
propagation step, in the spirit of GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt} rather than
hypergraph convolution, is
\begin{equation}
\tilde{e}_q = \tanh\bigl(W_q e_q + W_c\,\bar{A}_{q,:}\,E_c\bigr),
\label{eq:qkc}
\end{equation}
implemented with a single shared matrix ($W_q{=}W_c{=}W$) on the sum
$e_q+\bar{A}_{q,:}E_c$, then a separate input projection into the LSTM.
We do not claim a non-collapsible dual-map form: the tied-$W$
implementation is equivalent to one linear on the sum. Attribution of the
incidence \emph{message} uses a capacity-matched twin that keeps the same
question pathway and zeros only $\bar{A}$
(\texttt{--question-incidence-control zero}); an older hard-off twin that
removed \texttt{question\_embed} and the projection pathway is
capacity-mismatched and is reported only as a pathway-off baseline
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}).
Interaction embeddings
of $(c_t,y_t)$ plus Rasch-style item parameters feed an LSTM. The next
step is scored by reading a query vector at the \emph{known} identity of
item/KC $t{+}1$ (standard next-step KT: the model is told \emph{what} will
be asked, not whether the learner will be correct).

When \texttt{--time-gap} is on, a dedicated linear map of
$\log(1+\Delta t)$ can be added on the LSTM input, on the next-step
query, or both. We treat the prediction time as \emph{attempt-onset
correctness prediction}: the score is produced when the learner opens
item $t{+}1$, whose identity is already the query, not when the system
first recommends that item. LSTM-side $\Delta t$ uses only gaps between
already-observed steps (\emph{history-only}). The query-side gap is the
wait until the scored item; that item's identity is part of the
prediction request, so the gap is attempt context rather than a post-hoc
peek at duration after $y_{t+1}$ is observed. We do not feed the scored
step's own response into the query. We report a history-only $\Delta t$
row (\texttt{--time-gap-mode lstm}): fold~0 seed~42 test AUC $0.8289$,
$\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00170$ (FAIL; Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m},
Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}). Query-only also fails
($+0.00069$); only \texttt{both} passes. Per-concept exponential
forgetting is a pre-specified alternative that fails the gate (P4).
HypergraphConv on multi-KC item hyperedges, hint-item hypergraphs,
dual-gated graph-only $x_t$, and the graph-sensitivity auxiliary loss
$L_{\mathrm{aux}}$ belong to the v2 diagnostic encoder
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}) or to FAIL arms
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}), not to Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc}.

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:reuse}
Following \cite{daominh2026p0}, XES3G5M \cite{liu2023xes3g5m} is the

\subsection{Baselines reused from P0}
\label{sec:baselines-reuse}
Sequence baselines (DKT \cite{piech2015dkt}, AKT \cite{ghosh2020akt},
simpleKT \cite{liu2023simplekt}, GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt},
DyGKT \cite{dygkt2024}, DGEKT \cite{dgekt2024}) are trained
with the vendored \textsc{pyKT} implementations \cite{liu2022pykt} under

We reuse \cite{daominh2026p0}'s published results for BKT
\cite{corbett1995bkt}, GKT \cite{nakagawa2019gkt}, and SKT
\cite{tong2020skt}
under train-only graph construction (vendored CSVs at commit
\texttt{5e3d6a0}), except GIKT, AKT, simpleKT, DKT, DyGKT, and DGEKT on
XES3G5M, which we retrain under the clean $L{=}400$ protocol
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). GIKT shares DH$^2$-KT's compute envelope
(batch 16, epoch cap 30, patience 5); AKT and simpleKT inherit
\textsc{pyKT}/P0 defaults with only $L$ raised to 400. That is a matched
\emph{compute} budget, not a matched tuning search
(Appendix~\ref{app:hparams}; cf.\ \cite{pineau2021repro}). A
config-checksum script confirms the
XES3G5M P0 config match. The companion PDF is available to reviewers as
supplementary material on request.

\subsection{Clean evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:clean-protocol}
Headline AUCs use a \emph{clean} protocol aligned with \textsc{pyKT}'s
repeat-format warning \cite{liu2022pykt,liu2023simplekt}: the library's
KC-level export expands each multi-KC question into consecutive rows that
share the same timestamp and answer, so a large share of eval positions are
answerable by copying the input label. We therefore mask repeat-row targets
(\texttt{--mask-repeats}) in both our models and retrained pyKT baselines,
use chunked windows (\texttt{--window-mode chunked}) with
$L{=}400$, and report test-only AUC on the remaining scored positions.
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} uses a shared pyKT-aligned intersection of
$n_{\mathrm{scored}}{=}1{,}093{,}720$ positions across all listed models.
Native DH$^2$ scoring of the same clean mask yields
$1{,}093{,}755$ positions (difference of 35); calibration
(Table~\ref{tab:calibration-xes3g5m}) reports the native count. We do
not collapse multi-KC rows back to attempt level for the headline AUC:
each masked KC-row target is scored once. The
6{,}413{,}353 KC-expanded rows versus 5{,}549{,}635 recorded attempts in
\cite{liu2023xes3g5m} is the same multi-KC expansion pyKT warns about;
we score the masked remainder, not every expanded row.
Published P0 numbers that score every KC row are not comparable to this
table. Our contribution is a fair retrain under the shared masking rules,
not the discovery of KC-level row repeats.

\subsection{Credit gate}
\label{sec:credit-gate}
A knowledge type is credited only if the pre-specified
$\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ its matched twin. This is an internal
convention, not a timestamped public registry. Five-seed validation AUC SD
is $0.00015$ on the recommended tracer and $0.00023$ on Q$\leftarrow$KC
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} arms); $+0.002$ is $\approx$9--13$\times$
those SDs. For Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence the matched twin is
capacity-matched zero incidence (Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}): mean
$\Delta\mathrm{val}{=}{-}0.00010{\pm}0.00034$ over five seeds ($0/5$ PASS),
so incidence content is not credited. A historical hard-off comparison that
removed the whole question pathway is capacity-mismatched and is not a
credit test. The five-seed timing credit test remains: Linear $\Delta t$
vs.\ Q$\leftarrow$KC mean $+0.00274$ ($t_4{=}23.99$,
$p{=}8.95{\times}10^{-6}$); Holm--Bonferroni at $\alpha{=}0.05$ among the
remaining XES credit arms leaves this timing test significant. The six
FAIL rows in Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge} remain single-seed and are
judged only by the $+0.002$ gate, not by $p$-values: the gate is a credit
threshold anchored to seed SD, not a claim of simultaneous significance.

\subsection{Manipulation check}
A hypergraph ablation is interpretable only if destroying the constructed
hyperedges moves AUC. Near-total node-drop $p{=}0.90$ on the graph-only
diagnostic encoder (Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}) confirms
\emph{dependence} on some graph input. Degree-preserving rewiring asks
whether that encoder also uses \emph{which} concepts co-occur: it passes
on folds~0--2 ($\Delta$AUC $0.0318$/$0.0341$/$0.0351$; clean AUC
$\approx 0.75$, matched to node-drop;
Table~\ref{tab:manipulation}). Relation-label permutation is near-null
on every fold (expected for a single-kind encoder). An earlier
pairwise$+$exercise path was graph-inert ($\Delta$AUC$\approx 0.0003$).
The recommended tracer does not take this check: concept-stack
\texttt{HypergraphConv} is off, and Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence is a
bipartite membership matrix rather than a new multi-way graph.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}
Headline AUCs (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}) are clean $L{=}400$,
test-only. Q$\leftarrow$KC$+\Delta t$ is also on folds~1--2;
compute-matched GIKT/AKT remain fold~0. Other baselines come from reused
P0 tables on the older budget. The GIKT edge is small; we report
learner-level bootstrap CIs, not a SOTA headline. HypergraphConv and
hint-item hyperedges fail the pre-specified gate, so we do not credit
multi-way message passing. The node-drop check
(Appendix~\ref{app:graph-only}) is run on the graph-only diagnostic
encoder, not on the recommended tracer. Node-drop confirms
\emph{dependence} on some graph input. Degree-preserving rewiring on
folds~1--2 additionally moves AUC ($0.0341$/$0.0351$), which is evidence
that this diagnostic encoder is sensitive to co-occurrence structure, not
only to the presence of nodes. That check still does not support
graph-structure claims about QKC-T. Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence is a
bipartite membership matrix, not a new multi-way graph.

\paragraph{Deployment availability of the query-side gap.}
Attempt-onset scoring (this paper's protocol) produces $P(y_{t+1}{=}1)$
when the learner opens item $t{+}1$, so the query-side wait has already
elapsed. History-only / recommend-before-return uses only LSTM-side gaps
already in the log; that arm is the history-only row (test AUC $0.8289$,
FAIL; Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}). Serving a next item before the
learner returns is earlier still: the wait has not occurred, so
query-side $\Delta t$ must be omitted or replaced by a planned-gap proxy
we do not evaluate. The recommended tracer therefore assumes attempt-onset
availability; a history-only deployment should use the LSTM-only arm and
must not claim the $+0.00280$ both-mode credit.

\subsection{Interpretation of the empirical findings}
\label{sec:auc-tradeoff}
The results answer an attribution question, not ``hypergraph NNs beat
GIKT.'' Each paragraph below asks whether one knowledge type survived
the pre-specified ablation, and what that means for a system builder.

\paragraph{Question--KC incidence.}
Does not survive as credited structural knowledge under the
capacity-matched twin ($0/5$ PASS; Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}). The
recommended tracer still includes the GIKT-class question pathway for
architecture parity, but the train-only incidence message itself is not
what the gate attributes.

\paragraph{Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$.}
Survives on this backbone when both LSTM-side and query-side gaps are
present ($5/5$ PASS). History-only $\Delta t$ fails the gate
(Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}): the credited timing is attempt-onset
context, not a history-only forgetting cue.

\paragraph{Hypergraph convolution and path-derived concept groups.}
Do not survive as predictive knowledge: \texttt{HypergraphConv} on chain
hyperedges fails the gate (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Those
groups remain an audit/diagnostic artefact. The $\approx$8 point gap
versus GKT is the cost of the graph-only diagnostic encoder, not of
QKC-T.

\paragraph{Hint-item hyperedges, full Q-matrix, teacher grouping, expert
DAG, exponential forgetting.}
Do not survive (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Session+Hint on
FoundationalASSIST ($0.720\rightarrow 0.722$) is a small local shift, not
credited as multi-way graph novelty.

The Critic survey in Section~\ref{sec:tier2} is exploratory and is not
part of this attribution map.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges remain
  data-blocked; session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST shift AUC
  $0.720\rightarrow 0.722$ under concept projection, but the pre-specified
  hint-item hypergraph arm fails the $+0.002$ gate (L in
  Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}) and is not claimed as hypergraph
  novelty.
  \item Appendix~\ref{app:ate} reports an IPW hint check with weak
  positivity; it is not a contribution and must not be read as a
  recommendation against hints.
  \item The hyperedge leakage audit's pairwise-projection approximation
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}) weights all projected pairs equally; a
  hyperedge-native statistic is future work.
  \item Exploratory Critic survey: one fold, one local 7B LLM. Blinded
  Likert on the recommended tracer ($n{=}100$, 3 raters) is reported in
  Table~\ref{tab:human-eval}; ordinal Krippendorff $\alpha$ is $0.52$
  (faithfulness) / $0.56$ (usefulness). Mean pairwise Spearman is moderate
  ($\rho{\approx}0.43$/$0.34$) while within$\pm$1 agreement is high
  (${\approx}95\%$), consistent with compressed high scores. An earlier
  dual-rater $n{=}40$ pack on the v2 graph-only checkpoint is archived
  only. The grounded--IG flag gap is largely definitional because IG
  withholds $P(\mathrm{correct})$ from the Diagnostician while the Critic
  scores against that number. ID-anchored KC-Jaccard is not synonym-aware
  (XES3G5M lacks an in-repo KC name vocabulary); the JC gap is
  $\approx{+}0.010$. An LLM-scale sensitivity sweep remains optional
  future work.
  \item Reproduction environment: Windows workstation, single RTX~3090;
  unit tests pass in the released codebase.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and future work}
% =============================================================================
We presented DH$^2$A-KT as leakage-audited knowledge attribution: which
structural and temporal knowledge survives a controlled ablation.
Linear $\Delta t$ survives on the QKC-T backbone; capacity-matched
zeroing of the question--KC incidence message fails, as do hypergraph
convolution and several metadata sources. On XES3G5M (clean $L{=}400$,
test-only), the recommended tracer reaches $0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ over five
training seeds on fold~0 versus compute-matched GIKT
$0.8258{\pm}0.0001$ ($0.8295{\pm}0.0004$ over three learner folds).
History-only $\Delta t$ does not pass the gate; only LSTM+query timing
does. Future work: a tuning search for GIKT/AKT at $L{=}400$, and
hyperedge-native leakage statistics.

\section{Observational hint identification check}
\label{app:ate}
\label{sec:causal}
On FoundationalASSIST we report a standard inverse-propensity (IPW)
average treatment effect of $\texttt{hint\_used}_t$ on
$\texttt{correct}_{t+1}$, with logistic propensity on pre-$t$ summaries
and clipping. Unconfoundedness is assumed, not proven. Same-step
\texttt{discrete\_score} is not the outcome (it is mechanically tied to
hint requests). No estimate is reported on XES3G5M (no hint logs). This
check is not a contribution.

Using train transitions, the IPW estimate of hint use on \emph{next-step}
correctness is $-0.207$ (naive mean difference $-0.255$; trimmed IPW
$-0.211$; bootstrap 95\% CI $[-0.230,\,-0.187]$ on 50k-row resamples;
$n_{\mathrm{treated}}{=}36{,}151$). Propensity scores hit the clip floor
($[0.05,\,0.219]$), indicating weak overlap. The negative sign reflects
that weaker learners request more hints; it must \emph{not} be read as
the effect of providing hints, and it is not a recommendation against
hints. We did not compute Rosenbaum bounds or an E-value.
