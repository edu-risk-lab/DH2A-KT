\begin{abstract}
Graph-augmented knowledge tracing (KT) often constructs auxiliary
knowledge without a leakage audit, then reports a single AUC as if every
graph component contributed. This paper asks a leakage-audited
attribution question: which structural and temporal knowledge survives a
controlled ablation? DH$^2$A-KT is an attribution pipeline that is
tractable on a single consumer GPU (the $^2$ marks heterogeneous
hypergraph \emph{interfaces}; QKC-T and Zero$+\Delta t$ do not use
hypergraph convolution). It reuses the train-only protocol of companion
paper P0, extends the audit to hyperedges, and then ablates which
knowledge predicts. A pre-specified operating rule
($\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ a matched twin; five paired
training seeds) is met when a Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ \emph{branch}
is added---including on a capacity-matched zero-incidence twin
($5/5$ seeds pass; mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00255$)---whereas zeroing
only the train-only question--KC incidence \emph{message} is not
($0/5$ seeds pass; mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{\approx}{-}0.0001$).
Those timing twins change both the gap input and extra linear maps.
Architecture-matched T-zero / T-misaligned controls on the same maps
do not meet the $+0.002$ val rule ($0/5$; mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00183$
vs.\ T-zero; Table~\ref{tab:a1-timing}): aligned $\Delta t$ is not
isolated from the added branch. History-only LSTM $\Delta t$ does not
meet the $5/5$ rule ($2/5$ seeds pass): the surviving package uses
attempt-onset (\texttt{both}) scoring. QKC-T is the observed-incidence evaluation arm
for GIKT-family parity; Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal control
(test-only AUC $0.8295{\pm}0.0001$ on
$n{=}1{,}093{,}755$; Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}).
Baselines share a training envelope with GIKT (batch 16, cap 30,
patience 5; \ref{app:hparams}), not a matched hyperparameter search.
On that contextual benchmark GIKT is $0.8258{\pm}0.0001$
($n{=}1{,}093{,}720$). The same QKC-T recipe on three learner folds is
$0.8295{\pm}0.0004$. Teacher grouping and exponential forgetting do not
meet the five-seed rule; HypergraphConv and remaining metadata remain
single-seed diagnostics.
A graph-only diagnostic encoder (AUC $0.752$) is in the appendix.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% =============================================================================

Knowledge tracing (KT) estimates a learner's latent mastery of knowledge
components (KCs) from interaction history, supporting adaptive practice,
intelligent tutoring, and curriculum recommendation. The field has
progressed from probabilistic and sequence models
\cite{piech2015dkt} to graph-augmented models that propagate information
over auxiliary concept graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt}, and, most
recently, toward hypergraph representations that capture higher-order
relations among knowledge components \cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and
dynamic, temporally-evolving graph structure \cite{dygkt2024}.

This paper builds on \cite{daominh2026p0}'s train-only leakage protocol.
DH$^2$A-KT is a leakage-audited \emph{attribution} setting: construct,
audit, then ask which knowledge predicts. The evaluation arm QKC-T is a GIKT-family LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC
incidence and a linear $\Delta t$ branch
(Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal
control. Teacher, forum, and video members remain interfaces.
Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture} is the pipeline.

\subsection{Motivation}
Two concrete gaps motivate this work:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Higher-order structure is under-audited in graph-KT.}
  Hypergraph-KT models \cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} group multiple
  knowledge components into a single hyperedge, yet published systems
  rarely audit whether those hyperedges were constructed without leakage,
  and few expose an interface for entities beyond KCs (hints, teachers,
  forums, video) when such logs become available.
  \item \textbf{Graph provenance is rarely audited.} \cite{daominh2026p0}
  shows that pooling a knowledge-component graph from the full interaction
  log before a learner-based train/test split lets held-out information
  reach a graph-augmented KT model even when the sequence optimizer itself
  trains only on the training fold. \cite{daominh2026p0}'s protocol audits
  this channel for \emph{pairwise} prerequisite/similarity edges; published
  hyperedge-level audits remain scarce.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Questions}
\label{sec:rqs}
This work is motivated by a broader question: \emph{which leakage-audited
knowledge actually improves next-step KT?} We decompose this into two
questions, plus entities this paper does not claim to have learned:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RQ1} (\S\ref{sec:results}; Tables~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}--\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}):
  Under a leakage audit, which structural knowledge meets a matched-twin
  gate on this backbone?
  \item \textbf{RQ2} (\S\ref{sec:time-result}; Tables~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt},~\ref{tab:a1-timing}):
  Does the timing-branch gain survive under history-only input, and is
  aligned $\Delta t$ isolated from the extra maps?
\end{itemize}
An exploratory Critic-gated pilot over frozen scores is reported after
the predictive results (Section~\ref{sec:tier2}); it is not a third
research question and not a contribution.
Teacher, forum/discussion, and video nodes are
defined in the schema (Section~\ref{sec:prelim}) but remain data-blocked
on every corpus we use (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}): this paper
establishes an interface for those entities, not empirical evidence of
how they improve KT. A graph-only encoder and three-fold manipulation
check remain available as a forced-reliance diagnostic
(\ref{app:graph-only}); they are not RQ1's evaluation arm.

\subsection{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C1 (construction and audit).} A procedure that groups
  \cite{daominh2026p0}'s audited, acyclic prerequisite edges into
  \emph{path-derived concept-group} chain hyperedges (unordered member
  sets along directed paths; Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}) and names
  three leakage classes that a pairwise projection does not express
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext});
  session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST
  \cite{foundationalassist2026} (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}).
  Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention types remain data-blocked
  (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Construction is not claimed as an AUC
  lift.
  \item \textbf{C2 (which knowledge predicts).} An operating rule
  ($\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ a matched twin on five paired
  seeds; an internal convention, not a public registry) plus a
  repeat-target-masked KC-expanded protocol. Adding a Linear
  $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ \emph{branch} meets the rule on the QKC-T
  backbone ($5/5$ seeds pass; Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}) and on a
  capacity-matched zero-incidence twin ($5/5$; test-only AUC
  $0.8295{\pm}0.0001$; Table~\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}). Those twins add
  maps as well as gap input. T-zero / T-misaligned on the same maps do
  not meet the val rule ($0/5$; Table~\ref{tab:a1-timing}), so aligned
  $\Delta t$ is not isolated from extra capacity. Zeroing only the
  train-only incidence \emph{message}
  does not meet the rule ($0/5$; Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}).
  History-only LSTM $\Delta t$ is $2/5$ seeds pass and is not credited
  under the $5/5$ rule.
  QKC-T is the observed-incidence evaluation arm (five-seed test-only
  $0.8295{\pm}0.0001$; three-fold mean $0.8295{\pm}0.0004$);
  Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal control.
  Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} is a contextual benchmark: GIKT shares a
  training envelope (not a matched tuning search) and is
  $0.8258{\pm}0.0001$.
  Teacher grouping and per-concept exponential forgetting are $0/5$
  (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). HypergraphConv, hint-item
  hyperedges, a full Q-matrix, and a Junyi DAG subsample remain
  single-seed diagnostics below the gate.
  Graph-only v2 mean AUC $0.752$ versus GKT $0.834$ mixes encoder,
  budget, and protocol; it is not an isolated architecture cost.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Graph- and hypergraph-based knowledge tracing}
Pairwise KT graphs \cite{nakagawa2019gkt,yang2021gikt} and dynamic
variants \cite{dygkt2024} propagate over edges; hypergraph KT
\cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025} and heterogeneous node types
\cite{psykt2024,hhskt2023} still draw members from KC/exercise/learner
sets. Among the hypergraph-KT systems we read
\cite{thmn2025,hgkt2025,htkt2025,psykt2024,hhskt2023}, Teacher, Hint,
Forum, and Video appear as interfaces here rather than as evaluated
members. This paper's quantitative core is a GIKT-class LSTM with audited
Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); hypergraph
operators are single-seed diagnostics in
Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}, not five-seed credit tests.

\subsection{Forgetting and temporal effects in KT}
Sequence KT models have long treated elapsed time as a forgetting cue.
\cite{nagatani2019forget} extend DKT with multiple forgetting-related
features (lag from the previous interaction and past trial counts).
HawkesKT \cite{wang2021hawkeskt} models skill-to-skill temporal
cross-effects with a Hawkes-process kernel (WSDM 2021).
\cite{badran2025leakage} encode \emph{step} recency (how many KC-rows
since a concept last appeared) with learnable Fourier features; that is
not the wall-clock $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ we credit. Our P4 arm is
narrower: a per-concept exponential
$\exp(-\mathrm{softplus}(\theta_c)\cdot\mathrm{gap})$ on the same v4
backbone as Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$. That one form not meeting the gate does
not rule out power-law decay, data-driven half-lives, or Hawkes kernels;
we do not claim to have tested those alternatives.

\subsection{LLM agents and explanation faithfulness}
Closest in scope is \cite{ni2026ikteia}, which aligns LLM mastery reports
with implicit DLKT states and evaluates explanation faithfulness with
KC-overlap, an independent-generation control, and paired ablations.
Section~\ref{sec:tier2} is an exploratory pilot in that line, not a third
pillar: we gate rationales with a Critic over \emph{frozen}
$P(\mathrm{correct})$ and use ID-anchored KC-Jaccard because XES3G5M has
no in-repo synonym KC vocabulary. Broader LLM-agent
surveys \cite{llmedu2025,agentcat2026,graphaugmentedllm2025,graphrag2025}
motivate the frozen-core split: re-inferring mastery with a multi-agent
LLM over millions of interactions is not tractable on one consumer GPU.

\subsection{Leakage-controlled evaluation}
\label{sec:related-leakage}
We searched ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and Scopus through September 2026
with the terms ``hypergraph knowledge tracing'', ``leakage audit'',
``graph provenance'', ``KT label leakage'', ``question-level evaluation'',
and ``controlled attribution''. \cite{daominh2026p0} (companion P0, under review)
audits pairwise prerequisite/similarity edges under train-only
construction. Within that search we found no published protocol that
applies the same leakage diagnostics to \emph{hyperedges}; this paper
extends the audit from pairwise edges to hyperedges
(Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}). We reuse P0's train-only construction,
four pairwise diagnostics, and pinned artefacts
(Section~\ref{sec:p0-selfcontained}).
\cite{badran2025leakage} (arXiv:2508.17092) targets a different leakage
channel: KC-expansion \emph{label} leakage, via a MASK token in the input
embedding, plus Fourier recency (steps since a KC last appeared). That
work does not audit hyperedge provenance and does not run
capacity-matched attribution twins. Our clean protocol masks repeat-row
\emph{targets} at evaluation (pyKT-aligned) rather than replacing labels
in the embedding; Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ is wall-clock attempt
context, not step-count recency encoding.

Observational treatment-effect estimators in education
\cite{kaur2019causal,kumar2023causal,minn2022interpretable} are
background only.

\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}
% =============================================================================

Let $\mathcal{D} = \{(u, t, q, c, y)\}$ be a KT interaction log with learner
$u$, timestamp $t$, item $q$, knowledge component $c$, and correctness label
$y \in \{0, 1\}$, following \cite{daominh2026p0}'s notation. A learner-based
temporal split $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{train}},
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{val}}, \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{test}})$ assigns each
learner to exactly one fold (ratios $0.7/0.1/0.2$, three folds with split
seeds $42$/$43$/$44$, matching \cite{daominh2026p0}'s protocol so results
remain directly comparable, see Section~\ref{sec:reuse}).

\subsection{Self-contained summary of the reused P0 protocol}
\label{sec:p0-selfcontained}
Because \cite{daominh2026p0} is under review, we state the reused pieces
here. For each learner-based fold, $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ and
$E_{\mathrm{sim}}^f$ are built from \emph{train-fold interactions only},
then cycle-pruned to a DAG. Four pairwise diagnostics (P0 Table of
leakage metrics; computed on the train-only graph vs.\ held-out
learners) are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{flag}}$: structural learner-overlap
  indicator (whether any learner appears in more than one split);
  \item $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{overlap}}$: held-out pattern overlap on
  retained edges;
  \item $|\rho|$: magnitude of the Pearson correlation between edge
  weight and held-out outcome;
  \item $\mathrm{TBMR}$ (P0 also writes TBVR): \emph{within-train}
  temporal mixing --- the share of post-cutoff \emph{training} mass that
  supports retained prerequisite transitions --- \emph{not} a train/test
  membership violation.
\end{itemize}
P0 reports that switching from full-log to train-only graph construction
shifts baseline AUC by at most $0.003$ on XES3G5M, ASSISTments~2012, and
Junyi. We pin the companion repository to commit \texttt{5e3d6a0} and
copy its published baseline CSVs verbatim
(Section~\ref{sec:baselines-reuse}). The companion PDF can be attached as
journal supplementary material on request. Chain hyperedges in this paper
are bounded-length paths on the same audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
(Section~\ref{sec:tier1-graph}).

We extend this notation with a hyperedge $h=(\kappa,\{(\tau_i,e_i)\},f)$:
kind $\kappa\in\{\text{path-derived concept-group},\text{session},
\text{discussion-thread},\text{teacher-intervention}\}$, typed members
(student, exercise, concept, teacher, hint, forum, discussion, video),
and fold $f$. Chain hyperedges on audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}$ are audited
and used in diagnostic arms; they do \emph{not} enter QKC-T or
Zero$+\Delta t$ (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}). Teacher/forum/video
kinds are interfaces; session write-backs appear only in the Tier~2
pilot. QKC-T consumes train-only Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence and
timestamps (Section~\ref{sec:encoding}); Zero$+\Delta t$ keeps the
timestamps and zeros the incidence message.

\subsection{Heterogeneous hypergraph construction}
\label{sec:tier1-graph}

Path-derived concept-group hyperedges are bounded-length simple directed
paths on audited, acyclic $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (Algorithm~1). Each
hyperedge collects the concepts visited along a directed path, but
Algorithm~1 emits an \emph{unordered} member set, so path direction is
not retained in the hyperedge representation. This grouping is used for
audit and for optional \texttt{HypergraphConv} diagnostic arms; it is not
part of the QKC-T / Zero$+\Delta t$ evaluation arms.

\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE \textbf{Input:} $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$; bounds $[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]=[3,8]$
\STATE \textbf{Output:} path-derived concept-group set $\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{path}}_f$
\STATE Build directed graph $G$ from $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$
\FOR{each root $r$ with in-degree 0 in $G$}
  \FOR{each simple path $p$ from $r$ with $\ell_{\min}\le |p|\le\ell_{\max}$}
    \STATE emit unordered members $\{(\text{concept},c):c\in p\}$
  \ENDFOR
\ENDFOR
\end{algorithmic}
\noindent\textbf{Algorithm 1.} Path-derived concept-group hyperedges from
audited $E_{\mathrm{pre}}^f$ (member set is unordered; path direction is
not encoded). Chain length bounds $[3,8]$ match
\texttt{hyperedge.concept\_prerequisite} in \texttt{configs/xes3g5m.yaml}.
\label{alg:concept-hyperedge}

Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges are data-blocked on
the public KT corpora we use. Session+Hint hyperedges are built on
FoundationalASSIST only (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}). Chain
hyperedges on XES3G5M are audited and used in the graph-only diagnostic
(\ref{app:graph-only}), not as the QKC-T message-passing graph.

\subsection{Evaluation arms: LSTM with Q$\leftarrow$KC and time}
\label{sec:encoding}
The evaluation arm QKC-T is an LSTM backbone with train-only
question--KC incidence and a linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$ branch; no
convolution is applied on the concept stack. We keep QKC-T for
GIKT-family parity even though the incidence \emph{message} fails the
capacity-matched gate; the credited-minimal control is Zero$+\Delta t$
(Table~\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}). (Implementation flags
\texttt{--question-graph} and \texttt{--no-graph} turn incidence on and
concept-stack \texttt{HypergraphConv} off; Table~\ref{tab:aliases}.)
Path-derived concept-group chain hyperedges are not consumed on this
path. Let $A\in\mathbb{R}^{N_q\times N_c}$
be the train-only question--KC incidence, row-normalized to $\bar{A}$.
Each item $q$ has an embedding $e_q$; each KC $c$ has $e_c$. One
propagation step, in the spirit of GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt} rather than
hypergraph convolution, is
\begin{equation}
\tilde{e}_q = \tanh\bigl(W(e_q + \bar{A}_{q,:}E_c)\bigr),
\label{eq:qkc}
\end{equation}
the implementation we run (one shared $W$ on the sum, then a separate
LSTM input projection). We do not claim a non-collapsible dual-map form. Attribution of the
incidence \emph{message} uses a capacity-matched twin that keeps the same
question pathway and zeros only $\bar{A}$
(\texttt{--question-incidence-control zero}); an older hard-off twin that
removed \texttt{question\_embed} and the projection pathway is
capacity-mismatched and is reported only as a pathway-off baseline
(Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}).
Interaction embeddings
of $(c_t,y_t)$ plus Rasch-style item parameters feed an LSTM. The next
step is scored by reading a query vector at the \emph{known} identity of
item/KC $t{+}1$ (standard next-step KT: the model is told \emph{what} will
be asked, not whether the learner will be correct).

When \texttt{--time-gap} is on, a dedicated map
$f(x)=Wx+b$ of $x=\log(1+\Delta t)$
(\texttt{nn.Linear(1, hidden)}, bias on) can be added on the LSTM input,
the next-step query, or both. A raw gap of $0$ therefore yields $f(0)=b$,
not a proven zero hidden injection. We treat the prediction time as \emph{attempt-onset
correctness prediction}: the score is produced when the learner opens
item $t{+}1$, whose identity is already the query, not when the system
first recommends that item. We have not proven that the XES3G5M timestamp
is open/start time rather than submit/completion time; query-side
availability is therefore a deployment assumption, not a schema fact.
LSTM-side $\Delta t$ uses only gaps between
already-observed steps (\emph{history-only}). The query-side gap is the
wait until the scored item; that item's identity is part of the
prediction request, so the gap is attempt context rather than a post-hoc
peek at duration after $y_{t+1}$ is observed. We do not feed the scored
step's own response into the query. We report a history-only $\Delta t$
row (\texttt{--time-gap-mode lstm}): five-seed mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00193$
($2/5$ seeds pass; not credited under the $5/5$ rule;
Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}).
Query-only: $0/5$ seeds pass (mean $+0.00099$); only \texttt{both}
meets the rule ($5/5$). A per-concept exponential forgetting form fails versus Linear on all
five seeds (P4; mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{-}0.00252$;
Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}).
HypergraphConv on multi-KC item hyperedges, hint-item hypergraphs,
dual-gated graph-only $x_t$, and the graph-sensitivity auxiliary loss
$L_{\mathrm{aux}}$ belong to the v2 diagnostic encoder
(\ref{app:graph-only}) or to diagnostic arms
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}), not to Eq.~\eqref{eq:qkc}.

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:reuse}
Following \cite{daominh2026p0}, XES3G5M \cite{liu2023xes3g5m} is the
primary predictive benchmark: 18{,}066 learners, 7{,}652 items, 865~KCs
and 5{,}549{,}635 recorded attempts, which expand to 6{,}413{,}353
KC-level rows under the standard multi-KC expansion
(Section~\ref{sec:clean-protocol}). ASSISTments~2012 GKT AUC from P0 is
not reused as a comparison column. FoundationalASSIST
\cite{foundationalassist2026} is a secondary fold with synchronized
hints (Section~\ref{sec:foundational-assist}), not interchangeable with
classic ASSISTments~2012. Junyi supplies an expert DAG for structural
overlap only (Section~\ref{sec:junyi-gt}); we do not train the full
tracer there.

\subsection{Baselines reused from P0}
\label{sec:baselines-reuse}
KT reference baselines (DKT \cite{piech2015dkt}, AKT \cite{ghosh2020akt},
\textit{simpleKT} \cite{liu2023simplekt}, GIKT \cite{yang2021gikt},
DGEKT \cite{dgekt2024}) are trained with the vendored \textsc{pyKT}
implementations \cite{liu2022pykt} under our clean chunked protocol
(Section~\ref{sec:clean-protocol}). They are a \emph{contextual}
benchmark on the same protocol, not a claim that every baseline was
tuning-matched to DH$^2$.
We reuse \cite{daominh2026p0}'s published results for BKT
\cite{corbett1995bkt}, GKT \cite{nakagawa2019gkt}, and SKT

under train-only graph construction (vendored CSVs at commit

DyGKT \cite{dygkt2024} is cited as a related dynamic-graph KT system;
it was \emph{not} completed under this evaluator and is not a Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}
row.
GIKT shares DH$^2$-KT's \emph{training envelope}
(batch 16, epoch cap 30, patience 5)---matched limits, not measured
FLOPs or wall-clock. AKT and simpleKT inherit
\textsc{pyKT}/P0 defaults with only $L$ raised to 400
(\ref{app:hparams}; cf.\ \cite{pineau2021repro}).
The companion PDF is available to reviewers as
supplementary material on request.

\paragraph{Relation to P0.}
Train-only graph construction, edge provenance, and the pairwise DAG
audit in \cite{daominh2026p0} are reused and are not contradicted by
this paper. Downstream AUC columns in P0 that score every KC-expanded
row under an older evaluator are \emph{not} comparable to
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} and must not be read as a ranking against
QKC-T/Zero$+\Delta t$. The $\approx$8-point graph-only vs.\ GKT gap in
\ref{app:graph-only} mixes encoder, budget, and protocol; it is not an
isolated architecture cost. If a P0 claim depends on the older
evaluator, that claim should be updated on the P0 manuscript's own
track; this paper does not wait on P0 acceptance.

\subsection{Clean evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:clean-protocol}
Headline AUCs use a \emph{repeat-target-masked KC-expanded} protocol
aligned with \textsc{pyKT}'s repeat-format warning
\cite{liu2022pykt,liu2023simplekt}: the library's
KC-level export expands each multi-KC question into consecutive rows that
share the same timestamp and answer, so a large share of eval positions are
answerable by copying the input label. We mask repeat-row targets
(\texttt{--mask-repeats}) in both our models and retrained pyKT baselines,
use chunked windows (\texttt{--window-mode chunked}) with
$L{=}400$, and report test-only AUC on the remaining scored KC-rows.
This is \emph{not} claimed to be \textsc{pyKT}'s question-level
all-in-one evaluator \cite{liu2022pykt}: we have not fused KC scores of
the same attempt into one $p_a$. A fold-0 export trace finds
$208$ chunk windows that start on a globally repeated KC-row
(\texttt{a2\_infoflow\_trace.json}); we do not claim that a chunk
boundary never promotes a repeat-row into a first target.
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} reports DH$^2$ arms on native clean
positions ($n{=}1{,}093{,}755$) and pyKT baselines on the library
intersection ($n{=}1{,}093{,}720$).
Capacity-matched twins
(Tables~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}--\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}) and calibration
(Table~\ref{tab:calibration-xes3g5m}) use the native count.
An occurrence join on (user, dense-KC) matches $1{,}093{,}718$ rows;
$36$ DH$^2$ rows and $2$ pyKT rows remain unmatched, and $8$ joined
rows disagree on the label (\texttt{b6\_id\_join\_summary.json}).
The join is not a native attempt ID. Headline five-seed gaps stay
Zero$+\Delta t$ (native) minus the pyKT row (intersection) and are not
rebased onto the $1{,}093{,}718$ join.
Learner-level bootstrap CIs in
Section~\ref{sec:time-result} resample learners on one canonical
checkpoint pair; they measure sampling of learners, not training-seed
variance, and they do not use the occurrence join
(\ref{app:bootstrap}).
We do not collapse multi-KC rows back to attempt level for the headline
AUC: each masked KC-row target is scored once.
Table~\ref{tab:protocol-residual} records the already-measured residual
between scoring every exported KC-row and scoring the masked remainder
($+0.0101$ on QKC-T fold~0; $+0.0094$/$+0.0091$ on folds~1--2).
That residual is the pyKT repeat-format effect
\cite{liu2022pykt}, not an attempt-level collapse.
A no-$\Delta t$ Q$\leftarrow$KC residual of $-0.0007$ used
\texttt{window-mode last} and a different $L$ budget
(Table~\ref{tab:protocol-residual}): it does \emph{not} isolate the time
branch from the window rule. An older chunked no-time checkpoint
(\texttt{v4q\_clean}) has residual $\approx{-}0.0003$ and is not the
Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m} Q$\leftarrow$KC run.
On the fold-0 test export every repeat row has raw
$\log(1{+}\Delta t){=}0$ ($183{,}741$ rows; $14.4\%$ of unchunked
P0-protocol targets, not of the $n{=}1{,}093{,}755$ scored set).
Because $f(0)=b$, that is a zero \emph{feature}, not a proven zero
hidden state. We have not rebuilt an attempt-collapsed export
or an all-in-one question-level evaluator.
The 6{,}413{,}353 KC-expanded rows versus 5{,}549{,}635 recorded
attempts in \cite{liu2023xes3g5m} is the same expansion pyKT warns
about. Published P0 numbers that score every KC row are not comparable
to Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}. The retrain uses the shared masking
rules; it is not a claim that we discovered KC-level row repeats.

\subsection{Credit gate}
\label{sec:credit-gate}
A knowledge type is \emph{credited} only if every one of five paired
seeds has $\Delta\mathrm{val}\ge{+}0.002$ vs.\ its matched twin. This is
an operating rule locked for this ladder, not a timestamped public
registry, and it is \emph{not} the hypothesis $H_0{:}\,\mu_\Delta=0$.
A paired $t$-test on $\Delta$ answers whether the mean differs from
zero; it does not prove $\mu_\Delta>+0.002$. Comparing $+0.002$ to
training-seed SD after the runs is a post-hoc signal-to-noise
description, not evidence that the threshold was registered in advance.
For Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence the matched twin is
capacity-matched zero incidence (Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}): mean
$\Delta\mathrm{val}{=}{-}0.00010{\pm}0.00034$ over five seeds
($0/5$ seeds pass), so incidence content is not credited.
A historical hard-off comparison that
removed the whole question pathway is capacity-mismatched and is not a
credit test. Adding Linear $\Delta t$ vs.\ Q$\leftarrow$KC has mean
$+0.00274$ ($t_4{=}23.99$, two-sided $p{=}1.79{\times}10^{-5}$), and
adding it on the zero-incidence backbone
(Table~\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}) has mean $+0.00255{\pm}0.00015$
($5/5$ seeds pass). Those twins add maps as well as gap input; they do
not isolate aligned $\Delta t$ from extra capacity
(Table~\ref{tab:a1-timing}; $0/5$ vs.\ T-zero / T-misaligned).
Holm--Bonferroni at $\alpha{=}0.05$ is applied to an $m{=}3$ family of
two-sided paired $t$-tests on validation $\Delta$
(unrounded records in \texttt{b8\_holm\_credit\_tests.json}):
(i)~Linear $\Delta t$ on zero incidence
($t_4{=}38.01$, two-sided $p{=}2.86{\times}10^{-6}$,
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}{=}8.59{\times}10^{-6}$);
(ii)~Linear $\Delta t$ on observed incidence
($t_4{=}23.99$, two-sided $p{=}1.79{\times}10^{-5}$,
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}{=}3.58{\times}10^{-5}$);
(iii)~incidence observed versus zero
($t_4{=}{-}0.68$, two-sided $p{=}0.53$,
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}{=}0.53$).
(i) and (ii) remain significant versus $H_0{:}\,\mu_\Delta=0$; (iii)
does not. A previous draft printed Holm values computed on one-sided $p$
beside two-sided raw $p$; this draft uses one alternative throughout.
History-only $\Delta t$, P4, and M4 are judged only by the $+0.002$
$5/5$ rule, not by this $p$-family. The family was chosen after the
incidence and timing twins existed; we do not present it as frozen
before any exploratory run.
\input{tables/table_credit_stats}
Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge} single-seed rows (K, L, M1, M5) are
not five-seed credit tests; M5 is a 5k-user Junyi subsample with
unordered path-derived groups, not a directed-DAG test.
The gate is a credit threshold anchored to seed SD, not a claim that
every printed contrast was tested simultaneously.

\subsection{Manipulation check}
A hypergraph ablation is interpretable only if destroying the constructed
hyperedges moves AUC. Near-total node-drop $p{=}0.90$ on the graph-only
diagnostic encoder (\ref{app:graph-only}) confirms
\emph{dependence} on some graph input. Degree-preserving rewiring asks
whether that encoder also uses \emph{which} concepts co-occur: it passes
on folds~0--2 ($\Delta$AUC $0.0318$/$0.0341$/$0.0351$; shared clean
$0.7485$/$0.7511$/$0.7521$;
Table~\ref{tab:manipulation}). Relation-label permutation is near-null
on every fold (expected for a single-kind encoder). An earlier
pairwise$+$exercise path was graph-inert ($\Delta$AUC$\approx 0.0003$).
QKC-T and Zero$+\Delta t$ do not take this check: concept-stack
\texttt{HypergraphConv} is off, and Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence is a
bipartite membership matrix rather than a new multi-way graph.

\subsection{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}
Headline AUCs (Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}) are clean $L{=}400$,
test-only. QKC-T and Zero$+\Delta t$ are both five-seed test-only
($0.8295{\pm}0.0001$); three-fold QKC-T stability is in
Table~\ref{tab:auc-p0-dt-folds}.
Training-budget-matched GIKT/AKT remain fold~0. Other baselines come from reused
P0 tables on the older budget. The GIKT edge is small (${+}0.0036$ from
Zero$+\Delta t$) and is a matched \emph{training envelope}, not a matched
tuning search (\ref{app:hparams}). Learner-level
bootstrap CIs on one checkpoint are a secondary diagnostic of learner
sampling, not training-seed CIs, and they do not use the
$1{,}093{,}718$-row occurrence join. HypergraphConv and
hint-item hyperedges did not meet the single-seed gate, and teacher
grouping / exponential forgetting are $0/5$, so we do not
credit multi-way message passing from those arms. The node-drop check
(\ref{app:graph-only}) is run on the graph-only diagnostic
encoder, not on QKC-T. Node-drop confirms
\emph{dependence} on some graph input. Degree-preserving rewiring on
folds~1--2 additionally moves AUC ($0.0341$/$0.0351$), which is evidence
that this diagnostic encoder is sensitive to co-occurrence structure, not
only to the presence of nodes (shared-clean AUCs in
Table~\ref{tab:manipulation}). That check still does not support
graph-structure claims about QKC-T. Q$\leftarrow$KC incidence is a
bipartite membership matrix, not a new multi-way graph.

\paragraph{Deployment availability of the query-side gap.}
Attempt-onset scoring (this paper's protocol) produces $P(y_{t+1}{=}1)$
when the learner opens item $t{+}1$, so the query-side wait has already
elapsed. History-only / recommend-before-return uses only LSTM-side gaps
already in the log; that arm is the history-only row (five-seed mean
$\Delta\mathrm{val}$, $2/5$ seeds pass; not credited;
Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}). Serving a next item before the
learner returns is earlier still: the wait has not occurred, so
query-side $\Delta t$ must be omitted or replaced by a planned-gap proxy
we do not evaluate. The credited timing arm therefore assumes
attempt-onset availability; a history-only deployment should use the
LSTM-only arm and must not claim the both-mode $5/5$ credit
(mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}{+}0.00274$).

\subsection{Interpretation of the empirical findings}
\label{sec:auc-tradeoff}
The results answer an attribution question, not ``hypergraph NNs beat
GIKT.'' Each paragraph below asks whether one knowledge type survived
the pre-specified ablation, and what that means for a system builder.

\paragraph{Question--KC incidence.}
Does not survive as credited structural knowledge under the
capacity-matched twin ($0/5$ seeds pass; Table~\ref{tab:qkc-capacity}). The
QKC-T evaluation arm still includes the GIKT-class question pathway for
architecture parity, but the train-only incidence message itself is not
what the gate attributes.

\paragraph{Linear $\log(1{+}\Delta t)$.}
Survives on this backbone when both LSTM-side and query-side gaps are
present ($5/5$ vs.\ Q$\leftarrow$KC; $5/5$ vs.\ zero incidence;
Table~\ref{tab:qkc-zero-time}). Those contrasts add maps as well as
gap input. T-zero / T-misaligned keep the maps and do not meet the
val rule (Table~\ref{tab:a1-timing}). History-only $\Delta t$ does not
meet the $5/5$ rule (Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}): the credited package
uses attempt-onset scoring, not a history-only forgetting cue.

\paragraph{Hypergraph convolution and path-derived concept groups.}
Did not meet the single-seed gate: \texttt{HypergraphConv} on chain
hyperedges is not credited as five-seed evidence
(Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}). Those groups remain an
audit/diagnostic artefact. The $\approx$8 point gap
versus GKT mixes encoder, budget, and protocol; it is not an isolated
architecture cost of QKC-T.

\paragraph{Hint-item hyperedges, full Q-matrix, teacher grouping, expert
DAG, exponential forgetting.}
Hint-item HE, full Q-matrix, and the Junyi DAG did not meet the
single-seed gate. Teacher grouping and per-concept exponential forgetting
are $0/5$ (Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}).
Session+Hint on FoundationalASSIST ($0.720\rightarrow 0.722$) is a small
local shift, not credited as multi-way graph novelty.

The Critic pilot in Section~\ref{sec:tier2} is exploratory and is not
part of this attribution map.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Discussion-thread and teacher-intervention hyperedges remain
  data-blocked; session+Hint hyperedges on FoundationalASSIST shift AUC
  $0.720\rightarrow 0.722$ under concept projection, but the pre-specified
  hint-item hypergraph arm does not meet the $+0.002$ gate (L in
  Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge}) and is not claimed as hypergraph
  novelty.
  \item \ref{app:ate} reports an IPW hint check with weak
  positivity; it is not a contribution and must not be read as a
  recommendation against hints.
  \item Teacher grouping (M4) and per-concept exponential forgetting (P4)
  do not meet the $+0.002$ gate on any seed (mean $\Delta\mathrm{val}$
  $+0.00025$ and $-0.00252$). The remaining four single-seed rows in
  Table~\ref{tab:negative-knowledge} (K, L, M1, M5) are diagnostics
  below the gate, not five-seed credit tests.
  \item Calibration (ECE/Brier/NLL) is reported for QKC-T, Zero$+\Delta t$,
  Q$\leftarrow$KC, and a historical pathway-off twin
  (native $n{=}1{,}093{,}755$) and for GIKT/AKT/\textit{simpleKT} on the
  pyKT intersection ($n{=}1{,}093{,}720$), all seed~42
  (Zero$+\Delta t$ ECE $0.0072$). We report calibration as a diagnostic and do
  not treat ECE${<}0.05$ as a universal certification. History-only and
  query-only test-only means now use all five seeds
  (Table~\ref{tab:a7-query-dt}).
  The archived A2 combined val+test mean
  $0.8296{\pm}0.0001$ ($n{=}1{,}640{,}242$) is not a Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}
  cell.
  \item Repeat-mask residual on QKC-T is $+0.0101$ on fold~0
  (Table~\ref{tab:protocol-residual}). On the fold-0 test export,
  every repeat row has raw $\Delta t{=}0$ and $\log(1{+}\Delta t){=}0$
  ($183{,}741$ rows; $14.4\%$ of P0-protocol targets;
  \texttt{a3\_dt\_repeat\_dist.json}). Headline AUCs remain
  mask-target; $208$ chunk windows start on a globally repeated KC-row.
  We have not run attempt-level collapse or a question-level
  all-in-one evaluator. The DH$^2$/pyKT occurrence join leaves
  $36{+}2$ unmatched rows and $8$ label mismatches; headline $n$ is not
  rebased onto the join.
  \item We do not evaluate a planned-gap proxy for query-side $\Delta t$
  (the wait until a recommended item is actually opened). Deployment
  that scores before the learner returns must omit query-side $\Delta t$
  (Section~\ref{sec:threats}).
  \item The hyperedge leakage audit's pairwise-projection approximation
  (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-ext}) weights all projected pairs equally;
  the full-log control moves group-membership leak to $1.0$ but not
  sampled $|\rho|$. A hyperedge-native statistic is future work.
  \item Exploratory Critic pilot: one fold, one local 7B LLM. Blinded
  Likert on QKC-T ($n{=}100$, 3 raters) is reported in
  Table~\ref{tab:human-eval}; ordinal Krippendorff $\alpha$ is $0.52$
  (faithfulness) / $0.56$ (usefulness). Mean pairwise Spearman is moderate
  ($\rho{\approx}0.43$/$0.34$) while within$\pm$1 agreement is high
  (${\approx}95\%$), consistent with compressed high scores. An earlier
  dual-rater $n{=}40$ pack on the graph-only diagnostic checkpoint is archived
  only. The grounded--IG flag gap is largely definitional because IG
  withholds $P(\mathrm{correct})$ from the Diagnostician while the Critic
  scores against that number. ID-anchored KC-Jaccard is not synonym-aware
  (XES3G5M lacks an in-repo KC name vocabulary); the JC gap is
  $\approx{+}0.010$. An LLM-scale sensitivity sweep remains optional
  future work.
  \item Reproduction environment: Windows workstation, single RTX~3090;
  unit tests pass in the released codebase.
\end{itemize}

% =============================================================================
\section{Conclusion and future work}
% =============================================================================
We presented DH$^2$A-KT as leakage-audited knowledge attribution: which
structural and temporal knowledge survives a controlled ablation.
Adding a Linear $\Delta t$ \emph{branch} meets the five-seed operating
rule on the QKC-T backbone and on a capacity-matched zero-incidence
twin; capacity-matched zeroing of the question--KC incidence message
does not. Architecture-matched T-zero / T-misaligned controls on the
same maps do not meet the val rule ($0/5$), so aligned $\Delta t$ is
not isolated from extra maps. QKC-T is the observed-incidence evaluation arm;
Zero$+\Delta t$ is the credited-minimal control. On XES3G5M
(repeat-target-masked KC-expanded, $L{=}400$, test-only), both arms
reach $0.8295{\pm}0.0001$ over five seeds. Table~\ref{tab:auc-xes3g5m}
is a contextual benchmark: training-budget-matched GIKT is
$0.8258{\pm}0.0001$. Three learner folds of QKC-T mean
$0.8295{\pm}0.0004$. History-only $\Delta t$ is $2/5$ seeds pass and is
not credited. Future work: a question-level all-in-one evaluator;
five-seed repeats of K/L/M1/M5; and hyperedge-native
leakage statistics.

\section{Observational hint identification check}
\label{app:ate}
\label{sec:causal}
On FoundationalASSIST we report a standard inverse-propensity (IPW)
average treatment effect of $\texttt{hint\_used}_t$ on
$\texttt{correct}_{t+1}$, with logistic propensity on pre-$t$ summaries
and clipping. Unconfoundedness is assumed, not proven. Same-step
\texttt{discrete\_score} is not the outcome (it is mechanically tied to
hint requests). No estimate is reported on XES3G5M (no hint logs). This
check is not a contribution.

Using train transitions, the IPW estimate of hint use on \emph{next-step}
correctness is $-0.207$ (naive mean difference $-0.255$; trimmed IPW
$-0.211$; bootstrap 95\% CI $[-0.230,\,-0.187]$ on 50k-row resamples;
$n_{\mathrm{treated}}{=}36{,}151$). Propensity scores hit the clip floor
($[0.05,\,0.219]$), indicating weak overlap. The negative sign reflects
that weaker learners request more hints; it must \emph{not} be read as
the effect of providing hints, and it is not a recommendation against
hints. We did not compute Rosenbaum bounds or an E-value.
